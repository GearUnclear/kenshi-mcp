# Races

Stat and experience modifiers, hunger, speed, body-part health, gear restrictions and discrimination for every race a player character can be in unmodded Kenshi 1.0.x.

## Playable races overview
Also: starting races, character creation, race list, subrace, which race
- Seven races can be picked at character creation: Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek, Hive Prince, Hive Soldier Drone, Hive Worker Drone, Skeleton.
- Greenlander and Scorchlander are both "Human"; the three Hive races are "Hive" (Western Hive).
- Some game starts restrict the choice; many allow all seven.
- Race changes: experience rates, hunger rate, run speed, blood, bleed rate, heal rate, body-part health, which armour slots work, and how factions treat the character.
- Race never caps a skill. A penalty only slows how fast the skill rises.
- Other races can only join later as recruits: Soldierbot and P4 Unit (Skeleton subraces), Southern Hive Prince, Southern Hive Soldier Drone, Southern Hive Worker Drone.

## How racial experience multipliers work
Also: XP bonus, XP penalty, 1.2x, 0.8x, +20%, -20%, learning rate
- Each race has a multiplier on experience gained in certain stats. The usual values are 1.2x (+20%) and 0.8x (-20%).
- Exceptions: Scorchlander Dexterity 1.1x and Strength 0.9x; Shek Strength 1.1x.
- The multiplier applies to every XP gain in that stat, on top of the level multiplier and opponent bonuses.
- No race has a bonus or penalty for Melee Defence, Sabres, Hackers, Blunt, Polearms, Crossbows, Precision Shooting, Assassination, Swimming or Crossbow Smith.
- Weapon-class multipliers exist only for Skeleton (Heavy Weapons 1.2x) and P4 Unit (Katanas 0.8x).

## Choosing a race for a weak solo start
Also: best race, beginner race, easiest race, which race to pick
- Greenlander: no experience penalties at all, can wear everything, safe in Holy Nation land if male. Baseline for every number.
- Scorchlander: starts faster (17.9 mph against 15.7 mph at Athletics 0), +20% Athletics and Stealth, eats 10% less, heals 10% faster, treated as human by the Holy Nation.
- Hive Worker Drone: fastest starting runner (17.9 mph, top 31.3 mph), half hunger, +20% Athletics, Stealth, Thievery, Labouring. Fragile (75 health per body part, 50 blood), no boots, attacked or seized in Holy Nation land.
- Skeleton: never eats, 200 health per part, immune to weather. Needs costly Skeleton Repair Kits and is attacked on sight by the Holy Nation.
- Shek: 125 health per part and +20% Toughness and Melee Attack, but 1.25x hunger and tied for slowest runner.

## Greenlander
Also: human, greenlander stats, greenlander bonuses
- Experience: Cooking 1.2x, Farming 1.2x, Science 1.2x. No penalties in any stat.
- Hunger rate 1. Bleed rate 1 (highest of the playable races). Heal rate 1.
- Blood: 75 to 150, by the formula 75 + 0.75 x Strength.
- Every body part has 100 health.
- Run speed: 15.659 mph at Athletics 0, 26.844 mph at Athletics 100.
- Combat move speed multiplier 1. Vision range multiplier 1.
- Can wear every slot: Headgear, Shirts, Body Armour, Legwear, Footwear (not Hive shirts).
- No weather immunity.
- The Holy Nation treats Greenlanders as its own people; a male Greenlander has the easiest time there.

## Scorchlander
Also: human, scorchlander stats, scorchlander bonuses
- Experience bonuses: Armour Smith 1.2x, Weapon Smith 1.2x, Athletics 1.2x, Dodge 1.2x, Stealth 1.2x, Dexterity 1.1x.
- Experience penalties: Cooking 0.8x, Farming 0.8x, Labouring 0.8x, Strength 0.9x.
- Hunger rate 0.9. Bleed rate 0.9. Heal rate 1.1 (fastest-healing organic playable race).
- Blood: 75 to 150 (75 + 0.75 x Strength). Every body part has 100 health.
- Run speed: 17.896 mph at Athletics 0, 26.844 mph at Athletics 100 (same top speed as a Greenlander).
- Vision range multiplier 1.25. Combat move speed multiplier 1.
- Can eat Raw Meat.
- Can wear every slot, like a Greenlander.
- The Holy Nation treats Scorchlanders exactly like Greenlanders (both count as "Human").

## Shek
Also: shek stats, shek bonuses, horned race, shek hunger
- Experience bonuses: Melee Attack 1.2x, Toughness 1.2x, Strength 1.1x.
- Experience penalties (all 0.8x): Athletics, Dexterity, Dodge, Farming, Labouring, Robotics, Science, Stealth, Thievery.
- Hunger rate 1.25 (hungriest playable race). Bleed rate 0.9. Heal rate 0.8.
- Every body part has 125 health. Because healing scales with maximum health, a Shek regains health points about as fast as a Greenlander, but blood and robot limbs recover 20% slower.
- Blood: 75 to 150 (75 + 0.75 x Strength).
- Run speed: 15.659 mph at Athletics 0, 24.607 mph at Athletics 100 (tied with Skeleton for slowest).
- Swim speed multiplier 0.5: the slowest swimmer in the game.
- Headgear: all except Cap, Hachigane, Side-Angle Hachigane and Wool Hat. All other slots work.
- The in-game text claims natural armour; in practice it is only the 25 extra health.

## Hive Worker Drone
Also: hiver, worker drone stats, hive worker bonuses
- Experience bonuses (1.2x): Athletics, Dexterity, Engineer, Farming, Labouring, Stealth, Thievery, Toughness, Turrets.
- Experience penalties (0.8x): Cooking, Science, Strength.
- Hunger rate 0.5. Bleed rate 0.3. Heal rate 1. Blood 50.
- Health: head 125, every other body part 75.
- Run speed: 17.896 mph at Athletics 0, 31.318 mph at Athletics 100.
- Combat move speed multiplier 1.2. Vision range multiplier 0.5 (lowest in the game).
- Immune to acid weather and acid water.
- Can eat Raw Meat and Foul Raw Meat.
- No Footwear. Shirts: Hive shirts only. Headgear: most, with exclusions (see the helmets section).
- Limbs are easily lost because of low health; expect to need robot limbs.

## Hive Prince
Also: hiver, prince stats, hive prince bonuses
- Experience bonuses (1.2x): Athletics, Dexterity, Field Medic, Lockpicking, Perception, Science, Stealth, Thievery, Toughness.
- Experience penalties (0.8x): Labouring, Strength.
- The only race with a Lockpicking bonus.
- Hunger rate 0.5. Bleed rate 0.3. Heal rate 1. Blood 50.
- Every body part has 80 health.
- Run speed: 15.659 mph at Athletics 0, 31.318 mph at Athletics 100.
- Combat move speed multiplier 1.2. Vision range multiplier 1.
- Immune to acid.
- Unlike the other Hive races, Princes do not eat Foul Raw Meat.
- No Footwear. Shirts: Hive shirts only. Headgear: most.

## Hive Soldier Drone
Also: hiver, soldier drone stats, hive soldier bonuses
- Experience bonuses (1.2x): Melee Attack, Toughness.
- Experience penalties (0.8x): Armour Smith, Cooking, Engineer, Farming, Field Medic, Perception, Robotics, Science, Weapon Smith.
- Hunger rate 0.6. Bleed rate 0.3. Heal rate 1. Blood 50.
- Health: head 200, every other body part 100.
- Run speed: 15.659 mph at Athletics 0, 29.081 mph at Athletics 100.
- Combat move speed multiplier 1.1. Vision range multiplier 1.
- Immune to acid. Can eat Raw Meat and Foul Raw Meat.
- No Headgear and no Footwear at all. Shirts: Hive shirts only.
- With no helmet slot, the only dust-storm or gas protection available is Swamp Ninja Rags (20%).

## Skeleton (race)
Also: skeleton stats, robot, skeleton bonuses, oil, rebooting
- Experience bonuses (1.2x): Heavy Weapons, Robotics, Turrets.
- Experience penalties (0.8x): Dodge, Stealth, Thievery.
- Hunger rate 0: never needs food.
- "Blood" is shown as Oil: 100. Bleed rate 0.1. Heal rate 2.
- Every body part has 200 health.
- Run speed: 15.659 mph at Athletics 0, 24.607 mph at Athletics 100.
- Combat move speed multiplier 0.9. Vision range multiplier 1.25.
- Immune to all weather effects. Walks through water and acid at a fixed 6.711 mph instead of swimming.
- Armour only in Body Armour and Legwear slots: no Headgear, Shirts or Footwear.
- Healed with Skeleton Repair Kits (Robotics skill), not first aid kits.
- A knocked-out Skeleton shows "Rebooting" instead of "Unconscious".
- The Holy Nation attacks Skeletons on sight.

## Race comparison: hunger rate
Also: food consumption by race, who eats least, hunger multiplier
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: 0 (do not eat).
- Hive Prince: 0.5.
- Hive Worker Drone: 0.5.
- Hive Soldier Drone: 0.6.
- Scorchlander: 0.9.
- Greenlander: 1.
- Shek: 1.25.
- At hunger rate 1 a character uses about 40 nutrition per day, so a Shek uses about 50 and a Hive Worker about 20.

## Race comparison: body part health
Also: limb HP, limb health by race, max health, head health, chest health
- Greenlander: 100 for every part.
- Scorchlander: 100 for every part.
- Shek: 125 for every part.
- Hive Prince: 80 for every part.
- Hive Worker Drone: head 125, all other parts 75.
- Hive Soldier Drone: head 200, all other parts 100.
- Skeleton and Soldierbot: 200 for every part.
- P4 Unit: head 150, all other parts 200.
- A part is lost (limb) or fatal (head, chest, stomach) when it falls below minus its own maximum, so higher maximum health also means a deeper safety margin.

## Race comparison: blood and bleed rate
Also: blood amount, bleeding by race, oil, bleed multiplier
- Greenlander: blood 75 to 150, bleed rate 1.
- Scorchlander: blood 75 to 150, bleed rate 0.9.
- Shek: blood 75 to 150, bleed rate 0.9.
- Human and Shek blood = 75 + 0.75 x base Strength, so a Strength 1 character has about 76.
- Hive Prince, Worker Drone, Soldier Drone: blood 50, bleed rate 0.3.
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: oil 100, bleed rate 0.1.
- Hive characters bleed slowly but have so little blood that losing a limb (5 to 30 blood at once) can knock them out.

## Race comparison: heal rate
Also: healing speed by race, who heals fastest
- Heal rate multiplies the percentage of maximum health recovered over time.
- Greenlander 1. Scorchlander 1.1. Shek 0.8.
- Hive Prince, Worker Drone, Soldier Drone: 1.
- Skeleton 2. Soldierbot 3. P4 Unit 3.
- Because it is a percentage of maximum health, a Skeleton (200 health, rate 2) regains stun damage about 4 times as fast as a Greenlander.
- A Shek (125 health, rate 0.8) regains health points about as fast as a Greenlander.
- Heal rate also scales blood recovery.

## Race comparison: combat move speed and vision
Also: combat movement, vision range multiplier, sight range
- Combat move speed is how fast a character repositions during a fight. It is fixed by race.
- Hive Prince and Hive Worker Drone: 1.2.
- Hive Soldier Drone: 1.1.
- Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek: 1.
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: 0.9.
- Vision range multiplier: Soldierbot 2; Skeleton and Scorchlander 1.25; Greenlander, Shek, Hive Prince, Hive Soldier Drone, P4 Unit 1; Hive Worker Drone 0.5.
- A code-based reference gives the base outdoor vision range as 1500 game units (about 150 m), times this multiplier.

## Hit chances by body part
Also: where characters get hit, chest hit chance, stomach hit chance
- Each body part has a relative weight for being hit.
- Greenlander: head 80, chest 140, stomach 140, right arm 40, left arm 80, right leg 80, left leg 80.
- Scorchlander and Shek: same, except right arm 60.
- All Hive races: head 80, chest 140, stomach 60, right arm 40, left arm 80, each leg 80. Most hits land on the chest.
- Skeleton: head 80, chest 140, stomach 80, right arm 60, left arm 80, each leg 80.
- A part that was just hit becomes more likely to be hit again for a while (up to 4 times the weight).

## Which races can wear helmets and hats
Also: headgear restrictions, shek horns, hive helmet, skeleton helmet
- Greenlander and Scorchlander: all Headgear.
- Shek: all except Cap, Hachigane, Side-Angle Hachigane and Wool Hat.
- Hive Prince: most Headgear; the wiki's equipment guide lists the same four exclusions as Shek.
- Hive Worker Drone: cannot wear Crab Helmet, Flared Helmet, Karuta Zukin, Kusari Zukin, Masked Helmet, Spiked Helmet, Visored Helmet, Hachigane, Side-Angle Hachigane. One wiki page also lists Cap and Wool Hat; the Worker Drone page does not.
- Hive Soldier Drone: no Headgear slot.
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: no Headgear slot.

## Which races can wear shirts
Also: shirt slot, hive shirt, Hiver Chain Shirt, Leather Hive Vest
- Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek: normal shirts only (not Hive shirts).
- All Hive races: Hive shirts only. These are Blackened Chain Hive Shirt, Hiver Chain Shirt, Leather Hive Vest and Rusted Hive Shirt.
- Hive shirts are cheaper than normal shirts but give no limb coverage.
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: no Shirts slot.

## Which races can wear boots and footwear
Also: shoes, boots, sandals, footwear slot, acid ground
- Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek: all Footwear.
- Hive Prince, Hive Worker Drone, Hive Soldier Drone: no Footwear slot.
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: no Footwear slot.
- Footwear matters on acidic ground (Deadlands, Iron Valleys, Black Desert): unprotected legs burn. Hive races and Skeletons are immune to acid, so they do not need it there.
- Every race can wear Body Armour and Legwear.

## Weather and acid immunity by race
Also: acid immunity, weather immunity, which race is immune to acid, weather protection by race
- Skeleton, Soldierbot, P4 Unit: immune to all weather effects.
- Hive Prince, Hive Worker Drone, Hive Soldier Drone (and Southern Hive races): immune to Acid only.
- Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek: no immunity; they need protective clothing.
- Acid immunity also covers swimming in acidic water (Deadlands, Vain) and walking on acidic ground.
- Armour acid protection does not help while swimming in acid.
- Dust storms lower Perception by up to 20% and combat skills by up to 10 for affected races.

## Raw meat and Foul Raw Meat: who can eat it
Also: raw meat, foul meat, eating by race, cheap food
- Raw Meat (15 nutrition): edible by Scorchlanders, all Hive races and animals. Greenlanders and Shek must cook it first.
- Foul Raw Meat (10 nutrition): edible by Hive Soldier Drones, Hive Worker Drones and animals only. Hive Princes do not eat it.
- All other listed foods are edible by every race that eats.
- Skeletons, Soldierbots and P4 Units never eat.

## Swimming speed by race
Also: swim multiplier, water crossing, skeleton underwater
- Swim speed in game units = 6 + Swimming level x race multiplier x 0.38 (multiply by 0.2237 for mph).
- Race multiplier: Shek 0.5; Greenlander, Scorchlander and all Hive races 1.0.
- At Swimming 100: Shek 5.6 mph, humans and Hive 9.8 mph.
- Skeletons do not swim; they walk underwater at a fixed 6.711 mph at any skill level.
- A Shek at Swimming 100 is still slower in water than a Skeleton.
- Characters cannot drown.

## Holy Nation: treatment of Shek and Hive characters
Also: Holy Nation racism, non-humans in Holy Nation land, beasts
- The Holy Nation regards Shek and Hive characters as dangerous "beasts".
- A Shek or Hiver approaching Holy Nation towns or patrols without a human posing as their master is attacked or seized and enslaved (Rebirth is where captured non-humans are worked).
- Patrols do not attack Shek and Hive characters who are closely accompanied by a human squad member; guards assume they are servants.
- During an interrogation guards may demand the non-humans be handed over; how to answer is under "Bringing Shek or Hive characters into Holy Nation land" in Factions.
- Not being an ally, the safest plan for a non-human squad is to stay out of Holy Nation territory (Okran's Pride, Okran's Valley and neighbours).
- The Holy Nation is not at war with the Western Hive faction; the hostility is to the race inside its own land.

## Holy Nation: treatment of Skeletons and robot limbs
Also: Holy Nation skeleton, prosthetics, robot arm, attacked on sight
- The Holy Nation attacks any Skeleton on sight.
- A character of any race wearing a robot limb is treated the same way and attacked, unless Holy Nation faction relations are 50 or higher (allied).
- Even with relations at 50 or more, a character wearing robot limbs cannot talk to Holy Lord Phoenix, Paladins, Holy Priests or Holy Nation shopkeepers. Taking the limbs off restores this.
- Holy Nation trade caravans on the road can still be traded with.
- Holy Nation characters generally ignore anyone being carried: a human can carry a Skeleton or a robot-limbed character through their land.
- Skeletons are tied with Shek for slowest runner, so outrunning Paladins is hard early on.

## Holy Nation: treatment of women
Also: female character, gender, Holy Nation women
- Holy Nation dialogue is courteous to male humans and colder to female humans.
- A woman may trade and may keep Hive or Shek "servants" under control.
- In interrogations over non-human squad members, the chance to talk guards into letting them stay is 91% for a human male and 50% for a human female (with The Holy Flame and the right replies).
- Only male humans can hand in bounties to the Holy Nation for a relations increase.
- Human males carrying The Holy Flame are searched least often at gates.

## Hive characters cannot trade in Hive Villages
Also: hiveless, Hive Trader, Western Hive shop refuses
- Player-controlled Hive characters count as "hiveless".
- Hive villagers will not trade with a player's Hive character.
- Hive Traders refuse to sell to anyone in the player faction once they notice a hiveless character in the squad.
- To buy from Hive Villages (cheap robot limbs, Skeleton Repair Beds for c.800), send a Greenlander, Scorchlander, Shek or Skeleton and keep Hive squad members away.

## Shek Kingdom attitude to humans and Hivers
Also: flatskin, Shek racism, bug man
- Shek Kingdom characters look down on races they consider weak and harass humans ("flatskins") and Hivers passing through their cities.
- They are not attacked on sight for their race the way the Holy Nation attacks non-humans. (unverified)
- Shek respect strength; their bars and guards are otherwise open to all races. (unverified)

## United Cities attitude to non-humans
Also: United Cities racism, non-humans in the Empire
- The United Cities do not attack by race. Their nobility includes humans and Shek.
- Hivers there are mostly peasants (Empire Peasants are about 75% Hive).
- The United Heroes League, a vigilante group in United Cities land, targets non-humans for assault.
- The United Cities' real prejudice is against the poor: slavers and police take vagrants, beggars and escaped slaves of any race.
- Skeletons and Hive drones live among United Cities civilians.

## Cannibals, Fogmen and slavers: race differences
Also: who gets eaten, skeleton safe from cannibals
- Cannibals carry off unconscious organic characters to cages and later cook them. They do not eat Skeletons, though they still attack them.
- Fogmen carry off any non-Skeleton to prisoner poles to be eaten alive.
- Slavers enslave any race. A character who cannot wear shoes (Hive) is given Hive Prisoner Shackles.
- Enslaved humans have their hair shaved; enslaved Shek have their horns cut.
- Bandits who loot food take it from any race.

## Skeleton subraces you can recruit: Soldierbot and P4 Unit
Also: soldierbot stats, P4 unit stats, skeleton subrace
- Neither can be chosen at character creation.
- Soldierbot: 200 health per part, heal rate 3, vision range 2, Perception 1.2x; Dodge, Field Medic, Stealth, Thievery 0.8x. The one guaranteed Soldierbot recruit is Agnu.
- P4 Unit: head 150, other parts 200, heal rate 3. Bonuses 1.2x: Armour Smith, Engineer, Field Medic, Robotics, Science, Weapon Smith. Penalties 0.8x: Dodge, Katanas, Stealth, Thievery. Appears only rarely as a generic recruit.
- Both: no food, oil 100, bleed rate 0.1, all-weather immunity, armour in Body Armour and Legwear only, combat move speed 0.9, same run speed as Skeleton.

## Southern Hive subraces (recruit only)
Also: southern hive prince, southern hive worker, southern hive soldier
- Southern Hive characters can only join if bought or freed from slavery, and they spawn as slaves very rarely.
- Southern Hive Prince: bleed rate 0.2; bonuses as the Western Prince but without Lockpicking, Perception and Toughness; 80 health per part.
- Southern Hive Worker Drone: bleed rate 0.4; bonuses as the Western Worker but without Toughness; head 125, other parts 75.
- Southern Hive Soldier Drone: as the Western Soldier but without the Perception penalty; head 200, other parts 100.
- Hunger rates, blood (50), acid immunity and the no-Footwear rule match the Western Hive equivalents.

## Skeleton upkeep at a glance
Also: skeleton cost, repair kit price, wear damage, skeleton repair bed
- Cut damage on a Skeleton needs a Skeleton Repair Kit (average c.4,341, 500 charges) or an Authentic Skeleton Repair Kit (average c.1,865, 100 charges).
- Each weapon hit permanently lowers the struck part's maximum health by 4% of the damage, until the Skeleton sleeps in a Skeleton Repair Bed.
- Cheap repair beds: Black Desert City Skeleton Bar (c.200), Hive Villages (c.800). Free ones exist at Burn's Tower (Floodlands) and Iron HQ (The Crags).
- Two Skeleton Repair Kits always lie in the undefended Armoury Ruin in the south-east Deadlands.
- Details: "Skeleton repair: kits" and "Skeleton wear and tear" in Health and medical.

# Wildlife and hazards

Each creature and roaming squad in Kenshi (where it lives, group size, behaviour, speed compared with a running human, drops), plus weather and terrain hazards and how to survive them.

## How to read speeds and combat stats in this file
Also: run speed, mph, can I outrun, Athletics baseline, combat level
- Speeds are the in-game mph figures. A new Greenlander or Shek (Athletics 0, unencumbered) runs at 15.7 mph; Scorchlanders and Hive Worker Drones start at 17.9 mph.
- A Greenlander reaches 17.9 mph at Athletics 20, 21.3 mph at 50 and 26.8 mph at 100; a Shek tops out at 24.6 mph.
- Carrying weight (encumbrance) or a body slows a character below these figures.
- An animal's speed range (for example "8-21 mph") runs from a newborn to a full-grown, fast individual; treat the top figure as what an adult can do.
- "Combat stats" is the wiki's base attack/defence level for the creature (a new character is at about 1); animals also cannot be staggered, so they fight above their numbers.
- Squad sizes are the wiki's spawn ranges; per-region lists are in world-regions.md.

## What a new character can outrun
Also: escape, run away, too fast, slower than me, faster than me
- Slower than a new runner (15.7 mph): Swamp Turtle (4-8), Leviathan (4-9), River Raptor and Swamp Raptor (5-11), Iron Spider and Security Spider (6-12), Crab (6-13), Blood Spider and Skin Spider (7-14).
- Around the same or faster when adult: Land Bat (7-18), Gorillo (9-19), Black Gorillo (8-19), Bonedog (8-21), Goat (10-21), Skimmer (6-23), Boneyard Wolf (8-28), Garru (12-31), Wild Bull (9-31).
- The wiki still calls Skimmers "generally slower than a player character" and Gorillos slow enough to outrun.
- Cannot be outrun: Beak Thing (about 28-38 mph for half-grown to adult; Elders 55-59 mph).
- Humanoid bandits run at human speeds; starving ones have stat penalties.
- Blood Spiders follow swimmers underwater; most other pursuers do not attack in deep water.
- Train Athletics early: it is gained by running unencumbered with the run mode selected.

## Creatures and squads that eat downed characters
Also: eaten alive, devour, lose limbs, who eats you
- Beak Things: eat unconscious organic prey from the legs up and rarely stop.
- Blood Spiders and Skin Spiders: "eat" a downed victim by draining its hunger bar.
- Fogmen: carry victims to a Prisoner Pole in a Fog Deathyard and eat them alive, limbs first.
- Cannibals: carry victims to cages in a camp or village, then beat them to death and cook them when cages fill.
- Fishmen (Gurglers): carry injured organics to their nests, tie them to a pole and eat them.
- Skin Bandits: take organics to a Peeler Machine, which removes limbs and eventually kills.
- Bonedogs and Skimmers feed on corpses; Bonedogs do not eat living characters.
- Skeletons are not eaten by animals, Fogmen or Cannibals (they are still attacked).
- Rescue: another squad member must fight or lure the eater away.

## Squads that enslave or imprison downed characters
Also: slavers, enslaved, kidnapped, captured, cage
- Slavemongers (Slave Traders; roam the Border Zone and elsewhere): enslave any unconscious character they find.
- Slave Hunters: pick up unconscious characters and haul them to a slave location; sometimes attack innocents to sell them.
- Manhunters: stronger and more numerous; sometimes attack travellers and sell them as "escaped slaves".
- Manhunters do not target a squad that is allied with the United Cities or Traders Guild, that outnumbers them, that has police nearby, or that is inside a town.
- Reavers: capture victims as forced conscripts.
- Holy Mine overseers enslave characters knocked out near the mine gates.
- Dust King Tower: its wiki page says downed intruders are locked in cages on the third floor; the Border Zone page says they are simply thrown out. Either way they are not killed.
- In the Border Zone, slavers who pick up a dying character keep it alive.

## Squads that only rob or extort
Also: mugging, food stolen, pay tribute, protection money
- Hungry Bandits and Escaped Servants: search downed characters and take only food, then move on; weapons and armour are left.
- "Food" includes the raw meat inside a downed pet animal; removing it kills the animal.
- Hungry Bandit Leaders demand food before attacking; a small payment sometimes avoids the fight.
- Dust Bosses approach to mug: pay a sum of cats and be left alone, or refuse and fight.
- Black Dog squads demand protection money and attack if refused.
- Scavengers attack and loot anyone unconscious or dead.
- Bandits cannot take food that is on the ground or in a container; food in a backpack kept inside the inventory (not worn) is also safe from Hungry Bandits.

## Passive animals (fight only if provoked)
Also: harmless animals, safe to pass, herbivores
- Goat: peaceful; fights back hard for its size.
- Wild Bull: peaceful; defends itself with a charge attack.
- Garru: not aggressive but skittish; may attack if people move around very close to the herd. Standing still or keeping distance prevents it.
- River Raptor and Swamp Raptor: do not hunt; eat crops and corpses; fight back if attacked.
- Swamp Turtle: entirely peaceful unless attacked.
- Leviathan: not a hunter, but a loner sometimes attacks squads of 3 or more on sight.
- Pack Beasts, Pack Bulls and Bonedogs in a caravan defend their owners.
- Hungry Bandits often attack Garru for meat and lose.

## Bonedog
Also: wolf, bone dog, wild dog, dog pack
- Where: Border Zone, Okran's Pride, Okran's Valley, Skinner's Roam, Stenn Desert, Spider Plains, Vain, Bast, Hidden Forest, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Stormgap Coast, The Eye, The Hook.
- Groups: lone wolves in the Border Zone; night packs of 1-6; day packs of 1-7 in The Eye; swarms of 10-20 in the Bonefields; more around Wolf Dens.
- Behaviour: territorial; attacks creatures that come close to its patrol or nest. Eats corpses but not living characters. Picks up severed limbs to play with, then eats them.
- Speed: 8-21 mph; adults can catch a new character, pups cannot.
- Combat stats 20; heals itself over time.
- Drops: 1 Animal Skin, 4 Animal Teeth, 3 Raw Meat.
- Tame Bonedogs are sold by Nomad animal traders and some Farm Shops.

## Wolf Den (nest)
Also: bonedog nest, paw print on map
- A Wolf Den is a randomly spawning Bonedog nest, marked on the map by a paw print like every nest.
- Regions: Border Zone, Okran's Gulf, Okran's Pride, Okran's Valley, Skinner's Roam, Stenn Desert, Bast, Heng, Hidden Forest, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Leviathan Coast, The Eye.
- Contains bones and clutter and sometimes loot such as Animal Skins or weapons.
- Killing every animal of a nest triggers a notification; the nest then despawns and a new one appears elsewhere later.
- Nests near a player outpost send animal squads to attack it.

## Boneyard Wolf
Also: mountain wolf, big wolf
- Where: Bonefields, High Bonefields, Flats Lagoon.
- Groups: packs of 1-7; Boneyard Wolf Dens in the Bonefields.
- Larger, stronger brown cousin of the Bonedog; combat stats 40.
- Speed: 8-28 mph; adults outrun most characters.
- Drops: 6 Animal Skin, 8 Animal Teeth, 8 Raw Meat.

## Goat
Also: wild goats, bonegoat
- Where: Border Zone, The Hook, Hidden Forest, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Sinkuun, Shun, Stobe's Gamble, Sniper Valley, Greyshelf, Royal Valley, The Unwanted Zone.
- Groups: usually around 8 (spawn range 1-12 adults with 0-2 kids). No nests.
- Peaceful herbivore; combat stats 10, but a tougher fight than that suggests: a brand-new character who attacks one for meat is likely to lose.
- Speed: 10-21 mph.
- Drops: 2 Animal Skin, 7 Raw Meat.
- Sold by Nomad herders, Nomad animal shops and caravans; Goat Herder's Camps hold tame ones.

## Wild Bull
Also: bull herd
- Wild Bull, where found: Skinner's Roam (herds of 8-16 or 3-6), Okran's Gulf (3-6), Vain, Berserker Country.
- Peaceful; defends itself with a strong charge attack. Combat stats 20.
- Speed: 9-31 mph.
- Drops: 4 Animal Skin, 2 Animal Horn, 10 Raw Meat.
- Does not eat crops.
- Domesticated Bulls are sold at Holy Farms; Pack Bulls travel with caravans.

## Garru
Also: pack beast wild, garru herd
- Where: Border Zone, Shem, Vain, The Great Desert, Heng, The Hook, Shun, Bonefields, High Bonefields, Flats Lagoon, Leviathan Coast, Purple Sands, The Unwanted Zone.
- Groups: families of 2-6; herds of 6-21 in the south and on Leviathan Coast.
- Not aggressive but skittish: may attack if you move around right next to the herd. Stand still or keep your distance.
- An enraged Garru is sturdy (combat stats 30, large health pool) and routinely destroys Hungry Bandit squads.
- Speed: 12-31 mph.
- Drops: 3 Animal Skin, 10 Raw Meat.
- Tamed Garru are Pack Beasts: they carry a Garru Backpack and eat as much as two humans.

## River Raptor
Also: raptor, river duck, crop eater
- River Raptor, where found: Okran's Pride (most), Hidden Forest, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Leviathan Coast.
- Groups: 3-12; River Nests hold 3-12 teen and adult raptors.
- Passive scavenger: eats farm crops and corpses; fights back only if attacked and cannot be scared off crops without violence.
- Weak (combat stats 10) and slow (5-11 mph).
- Drops: 1 Animal Skin, 4 Foul Raw Meat.
- Holy Nation gate guards kill them often; the bodies can be looted.

## Swamp Raptor
Also: swamp duck, raptor herd
- Where: The Swamp, South Wetlands, Burning Forest, The Crater, The Unwanted Zone (aggressive mobs of 3-12 there), Raptor Island, Leviathan Coast.
- Groups: large squads; Swamp Raptor Nests hold 3-12 adults.
- Docile crop and corpse eater like the River Raptor but stronger (combat stats 30).
- Speed: 5-11 mph.
- Drops: 1 Animal Skin, 6 Foul Raw Meat.
- On Raptor Island they attack only if you stand near their nests.
- The Megaraptor (combat stats 60; drops 20 Animal Skin) lives in the ruined citadel on Raptor Island.

## Swamp Turtle
Also: turtle
- Swamp Turtle, where found: The Swamp (alone or in pairs, up to 3), South Wetlands (herds of 5-12), Burning Forest, The Unwanted Zone.
- Tall, entirely peaceful unless attacked; combat stats 10.
- Very slow: 4-8 mph.
- Drops: 14 Raw Meat, the most meat of any common animal.

## Beak Thing
Also: Gutter, beak things, giraffe monster
- Where: Vain, Gut, Bonefields, High Bonefields, The Crater, The Unwanted Zone, Greenbeach, Leviathan Coast; smaller numbers in Shem, Venge, Stormgap Coast, Flats Lagoon, Cannibal Plains, Purple Sands.
- Groups: 1-3 or 2-6; swarms of 6-14 in the Bonefields.
- Behaviour: attacks on sight; hits hard; eats incapacitated organic prey alive from the bottom up.
- Speed depends only on age: 19 mph newborn, 28.5 mph half-grown, 38 mph adult. No new character can outrun one.
- Combat stats 35; very high hunger.
- Drops: 6 Animal Skin, 12 Foul Raw Meat, 5 Raw Meat.
- Survival: avoid being noticed; sneak, especially at night. If spotted, break line of sight and sneak again, juke while running, or lead it into other enemies.
- A character eaten while merely knocked down (not in recovery coma) gets up at once and the Beak Thing flees.

## Elder Beak Thing and Nest of Beak Things
Also: beak nest, Beak Thing Egg, elder gutter
- Elder Beak Things are very old, larger individuals found only in the Bonefields and High Bonefields; speed 55-59 mph, the fastest creature in the game, and a faster attack.
- Escaping an Elder by speed is nearly impossible: bait an attack, then run behind objects to break line of sight.
- Nests of Beak Things spawn in Vain, Gut, Bonefields, High Bonefields, The Crater, The Unwanted Zone, Leviathan Coast and Cannibal Plains. The wiki's advice on seeing one is "Run".
- A nest holds 1-30 Beak Thing Eggs, a valuable trade item (about 4,000 cats each per the Gut page).
- Fighting them: one armoured character takes the hits while others shoot crossbows; Falling Sun and Polearm weapons do bonus damage.

## Gorillo
Also: white gorilla, ape
- Gorillo, where found: Vain (most common roaming animal there), Greyshelf, Shun, The Unwanted Zone.
- Travels alone. Aggressive to anything that passes.
- Combat stats 60: far above a new squad.
- Speed: 9-19 mph, slow and lumbering; can be outrun or kited with crossbows.
- Drops: 6 Animal Skin.

## Black Gorillo
Also: black gorilla, blue gorillo
- Black Gorillo, where found: the south-east: Stobe's Gamble and Sonorous Dark (most), Stobe's Garden, The Crags, Sniper Valley, Greyshelf.
- Travels alone; aggressive; combat stats 80.
- Speed: 8-19 mph; easy to outmanoeuvre.
- Drops: 8 Animal Skin.
- A larger variant, the King Gorillo, lives in a ruin in Stobe's Gamble.

## Blood Spider
Also: red spider, swamp spider, bloodspider
- Blood Spider, where found: The Swamp, Burning Forest, South Wetlands; small groups in Shem and Venge.
- Groups: 1-10; swarms of 14-30 young in Burning Forest.
- Behaviour: attacks on sight in groups; drains the hunger of downed victims. Thin and hard to see in swamp shadows.
- Fragile individually but hits very hard (combat stats 40).
- Speed: 7-14 mph: easy to outrun on open ground.
- Walks along the bottom of water and keeps attacking swimmers, so never flee into a lake.
- Weak to bleeding but recovers blood fast.
- Drops: 2 Animal Teeth. Looting the teeth from a downed spider kills it; do so before it wakes.
- The recruit Miu curses aloud when spiders are near: an early warning.

## Skin Spider
Also: tan spider, big spider
- Skin Spider, where found: Arach (squads of 1-10, swarms of 20-30), Spider Plains, Shun, Stenn Desert (1-3), Bonefields, High Bonefields.
- Behaviour: hunts anything nearby; high cutting damage; drains the hunger of downed victims like the Blood Spider.
- Larger and tougher than Blood Spiders (combat stats 40) but less dangerous, because it keeps to open terrain where it is seen early.
- Speed: 7-14 mph; an unencumbered character outpaces it.
- High bleeding resistance; wakes quickly after a knockout. Loot its claws to kill it once downed.
- Drops: 2 Animal Claw.
- Also walks through water.
- Polearms and Desert Sabres have damage bonuses that help.

## Skimmer
Also: sand bug, desert insect, skimmers
- Where: Skimsands (densest), The Great Desert, Heng, The Eye, Bast, Grey Desert, Sinkuun.
- Groups: loners that hunt at all hours; packs of 3-6 at night.
- Behaviour: highly aggressive; feeds on corpses. Nest Skimmers sleep buried in sand from 6:00 to 23:00 and ambush anything that walks too close; look for white faceplates poking out of the sand.
- Combat stats 35.
- Speed: 6-23 mph, but the wiki says they are generally slower than a player character.
- Drops: 6 Animal Claw, 8 Foul Raw Meat, 2 Raw Meat.
- Samurai gate guards kill them easily: leading a Skimmer to a United Cities gate is an early way to get meat and claws.
- Skimmers count as neutral to every town; guards act only once a Skimmer attacks someone they protect (meitouclient notes).
- Skimmer Nests spawn in Skimsands, The Great Desert and Heng.

## Land Bat
Also: landbat, bat swarm
- Land Bat, where found: Stobe's Gamble (squads of 1-10, swarms of 18-30), Stobe's Garden, The Crags, The Pits, Flats Lagoon.
- Behaviour: travels in packs; hostile to people who cross its path.
- Combat stats 30; cannot be staggered.
- Speed: 7-18 mph, not a fast runner; packs keep far from towns and are easy to avoid.
- Drops: 6 Animal Skin, 4 Animal Teeth, 3 Foul Raw Meat, 2 Raw Meat.

## Leviathan
Also: giant beast, leviathan pearl
- Where: Leviathan Coast (loners and families of 1-2 adults with 0-3 young); single loners in Bonefields, Stobe's Gamble, Sniper Valley.
- The largest living creature in the game; huge endurance and area attacks that hit a whole squad.
- Does not hunt people, but a lone Leviathan that sees a squad of three or more may roar and attack it (10% chance for a squad of 3, 30% for 4 or more). A family defends its members.
- Speed: 4-9 mph.
- Drops: 1 Leviathan Pearl (expensive).
- Because it is so slow, crossbow squads shoot it from a distance to train.

## Crab
Also: crabs, crab nest, crab egg
- Where: Howler Maze, Greenbeach, The Pits East, The Unwanted Zone, Fishman Island, Islands of None.
- Groups: single wanderers, groups of 5-10; Crab Nests hold 5-30 young and up to 49 Crab Eggs (for selling only).
- Usually hostile; a strong fighter (combat stats 35) but slow: 6-13 mph.
- Drops: 6 Raw Meat, 1 Foul Raw Meat.
- Crabs also spawn inside some buildings in Howler Maze.
- Crab Raiders bring pet Crabs to fights.
- The Megacrab (combat stats 50; drops 5 Megacrab Ganglion) patrols Lost Town in Howler Maze.

## Iron Spider
Also: robot spider, machine spider
- Iron Spider, where found: Deadlands, Iron Valleys, Floodlands, Obedience, The Iron Trail, Grey Desert, Cheaters Run, Forbidden Isle, Sonorous Dark, Ashlands.
- Groups: 1-2 in the Deadlands and Floodlands; 4-18 in Cheaters Run, Sonorous Dark and Ashlands; 4-12 on Forbidden Isle.
- Behaviour: attacks living things indiscriminately. Many inactive wrecks lie around, so a working one is hard to tell apart until close.
- Does not eat downed characters, but its maiming attacks leave lightly armoured victims to die of wounds.
- Combat stats 40; speed 6-12 mph, slower than any runner.
- Drops: 6 Iron Plates, 2 Electrical Components.

## Security Spider
Also: ruin guardian, lab guard, Security Spider II
- Where: inside Ancient Labs, Lost Armouries and other ancient locations; they rarely leave their rooms.
- Stronger than Iron Spiders: combat stats 60; spawn in groups; each attack can hit 2 nearby characters, so spread out.
- Do not eat; but a character who wakes up next to them is attacked again.
- Speed: 6-12 mph.
- Drops: 6 Iron Plates, 4 Electrical Components, 1 Skeleton Muscle.
- Security Spider II is a smaller, weaker model guarding the Black Desert City bar, Scraphouse, the Secret Drug Farm, several armouries and the Armour King's shop.

## Cleanser Unit and Mountain Dog
Also: robot leviathan, cannibal hunter dog
- Cleanser Unit: huge leviathan-like robot patrolling the Ashlands alone; combat stats 50, high health; speed 4-9 mph; drops 1 AI Core, 4 CPU Unit and many plates and components.
- Mountain Dog: larger Bonedog variant that accompanies Cannibal Hunter patrols (0-2 per patrol) in Cannibal Plains, Hidden Forest, Northern Coast and Sinkuun; combat stats 30; speed 8-21 mph; drops 2 Animal Skin, 4 Animal Teeth, 6 Raw Meat. It does not spawn wild.

## Looting and killing downed animals
Also: animal loot, raw meat from animals, animal skin, teeth, butchered
- An animal's meat, skins and teeth are in its inventory from the moment it spawns; they are not created at death (meitouclient notes).
- Taking one of these items out of a living animal that is down kills it; the game reports that it "has been butchered".
- Animals with self-healing or fast wake-up (Bonedogs, spiders) get up again if left; loot them promptly.
- The player's own animals carry these items too, but their inventory window hides them and they add no weight (meitouclient notes).
- Typical meat yields: Swamp Turtle 14, Garru 10, Wild Bull 10, Boneyard Wolf 8, Goat 7, Crab 6, Bonedog 3 Raw Meat.
- Foul Raw Meat comes from raptors, Beak Things, Skimmers, Land Bats and Gurglers.
- Animal Skin: Beak Thing 6, Boneyard Wolf 6, Land Bat 6, Gorillo 6, Wild Bull 4, Garru 3, Goat 2, Bonedog 1.

## Animal ages: Pup, Teen, Adult, Elder
Also: animal age, pup, elder, aged 70, animal growth
- Every animal has an age number: below 0.4 it is labelled Pup, below 0.6 Teen, up to 1.1 Adult, above that Elder (meitouclient notes).
- An animal's combat stats are multiplied by its age, and its size and health grow with age, so a Pup is far weaker and slower than the figures in this file.
- The "lifespan" value on wiki stat blocks is the number of game days from age 0 to age 1.
- Wiki spawn lists give ages as percentages: "Bonedog aged 70" is an Adult at 0.7; "Goats at age 3" are newborn kids; Elder Beak Things spawn at about 2.0.
- What each kind eats (meitouclient notes): goats, bulls, Garru and turtles eat food left on the ground; Bonedogs and Boneyard Wolves also eat corpses and play with limbs; Beak Things and spiders eat any body; raptors eat crops.

## How far enemies see you
Also: detection range, sight range, vision, sneak past, night travel
- These figures come from the meitouclient project's decompile notes, converted at 1 game unit = 0.1 m.
- Sight range outdoors is about 150 m, multiplied by a race factor (0.5 to 2; 1 for most races); inside a building it is 50 m.
- At night sight range falls to half. A dust storm cuts it to as little as 40%; other bad weather to about 75%.
- Characters see only in front: nothing beyond about 78 degrees either side of where they face.
- Sneaking or crawling targets are seen at half to three-quarters of the distance, less again with high Stealth; a watcher needs time to recognise a sneaker.

## How far enemies hear you and how chases end
Also: noise, hearing range, lose pursuers, shared sightings, escape a chase
- Figures from the meitouclient project's decompile notes, converted at 1 game unit = 0.1 m.
- A moving character is heard within 35-60 m depending on speed; a sneaker within 8-45 m depending on Stealth.
- What one squad member sees or hears, its squad mates within twice sight range know too.
- There is no fixed give-up distance. An NPC gives up when it has neither seen nor heard its target for 13.2 game minutes (about 24 real seconds at normal speed), or when it stands within 6 m of where it last sensed the target; never within the first 5 game minutes (about 9 real seconds) of the chase.
- It also drops a target that is knocked out, carried, caged or dead.

## Starving Bandit squads: size, stats and gear
Also: hungry bandit squad, starving bandit mob, Hungry Bandit stats
- Where: Border Zone (most common squads), Okran's Gulf, Okran's Pride, Okran's Valley, Skinner's Roam, Shem, Dreg, Northern Coast.
- Groups: 5-9 Hungry Bandits with 1 Hungry Bandit Leader (about 8 in all); big mobs of 9-16 (about 13); giant mobs of 40 with 2 leaders in Skinner's Roam and Shem.
- Combat stats: Hungry Bandit 5, leader 8, further reduced 10-15% by starvation. Weak singly, dangerous to a new character only by numbers.
- Gear: Iron Club, Rag Shirt and other rags, Shoddy grade.
- Clubs deal stun damage, so victims usually wake and recover.
- After a knockout they take only food and leave.
- Begging, paying them off and what they can steal: "Starving Bandits (Hungry Bandits)" in Factions.

## Dust Bandit squads: size, stats and gear
Also: dust bandit squad, Dust Bandit Bowman, dust bandit patrol, dust bandit loot
- Where: Border Zone and Shem (roaming squads); camps also in Skinner's Roam; HQ at Dust King Tower.
- Groups: 1 Dust Boss, 5-10 Dust Bandits, 0-4 Dust Bandit Bowmen. A Dust Bandit Camp holds 3 slightly larger squads.
- Combat stats: Dust Bandit 15, bowman 15, Dust Boss 25. The weakest organised bandits, but clearly stronger than a new character: avoid them or lead them to guards.
- Uniform: Spiked Helmet, Heart Protector, Armoured Rag Skirt, Samurai Boots; most carry a Horse Chopper (a sabre), some a Junkbow.
- They use cutting weapons and crossbows, so a defeat means bleeding wounds.
- They also fight Starving Bandits and are attacked by town guards.
- Their gear is early-game armour worth looting when guards or Shinobi Thieves kill them.
- The toll their Dust Boss demands, and how to answer: "Dust Bandits" in Factions.

## Dust Bandit Camp
Also: bandit camp, free camp beds
- Randomly spawning camp in the Border Zone, Shem and Skinner's Roam; several can exist at once, and they can appear close to The Hub.
- Holds 3 Dust Bandit squads, each slightly larger than a roaming squad.
- Has Camp Beds the player can use for free once it is safe.
- Looting the camp's items after its occupants are defeated removes the camp from the map.

## Slaver patrols: Slavemonger, Slave Hunter and Manhunter squads
Also: slaver patrol, slaver caravan, manhunter patrol, slave hunter squad
- Slavemongers (Slave Traders): squads of 1-8 in the Border Zone and elsewhere; combat stats 25; enslave unconscious characters and recapture escaped slaves.
- Slave Hunters: squads of 4-10 in The Hook and United Cities lands; weaker and worse equipped than Manhunters.
- Manhunters: patrols of 5-14 in The Great Desert, Heng, Skimsands, The Eye, The Hook; combat stats 21.
- All three mainly capture Starving Vagrants, Rebel Farmers and escaped slaves, but sometimes attack travellers.
- Slaver caravans (1 boss, 7-14 guards) also travel the desert roads.
- Whom Manhunters and Slave Hunters leave alone: "Manhunters and Slave Hunters" in Factions.

## Holy Nation patrols
Also: paladin patrol, Holy Army Patrol, Holy Army Force, inquisitor squad
- Where: Okran's Pride (very frequent), Okran's Gulf, Okran's Valley, Bast, Skimsands.
- Holy Army Patrol: 1 High Paladin, 4-6 Paladins, 6-10 Holy Servants. Inquisitor squad: 1 Inquisitor, 4 High Paladins, 4 Paladins. Holy Army Force (Bast, Skimsands): 3-5 Paladins, 6-8 Holy Sentinels, 30-45 Holy Chosen.
- Friendly to human men.
- Hostile to non-humans, Skeletons and characters with robot limbs unless these pass as servants of a human escort.
- A human male carrying The Holy Flame should do the talking; see "Holy Nation interrogations: what to answer" in Factions.
- They enter the Border Zone only after certain leaders die.

## Shek patrols and rogue Shek squads: sizes and stats
Also: Shek Patrol, Shek Scout Squad, Shek warriors, Kral's Chosen squad, Berserker squad, Band of Bones squad
- Shek Patrol: 1-2 Hundred Guardians, 4-8 Shek Warriors; Shek Scout Squad: 4-7. Stenn Desert and Spider Plains. They start unprovoked fights with squads allied to The Holy Nation.
- Kral's Chosen: squads of 5-8; combat stats about 36; harass and assault outsiders in the wild. HQ New Kralia.
- Berserkers: squads of 2-8; combat stats 35 and up (the faction page says 40+); attack on sight. Stenn Desert, Spider Plains, Cannibal Plains, Berserker Country.
- Band of Bones: squads of 4-8; combat stats in the 20s-30s; all carry Combat Cleavers; may randomly attack. Spider Plains, The Swamp, The Hook, Bonefields, Shem.
- None of these roam the Border Zone at the start of the game.
- Leaders, bounties and bases of the three rogue groups: "Kral's Chosen, Berserkers and Band of Bones" in Factions.

## Rebel Farmers and Starving Vagrants
Also: outlaw farmers, vagrant bandits
- Rebel Farmers: the commonest bandits of United Cities land (The Great Desert, Heng, Sinkuun, Bast, Skimsands, Stormgap Coast, The Hook, Grey Desert).
- Rebel Farmer squads: 1-6 Outlaw Swordsmen with 3-10 Outlaw Farmers and 0-2 Bonedogs; small squads of 1 swordsman and 3-6 farmers; heavy squads with a captain and 3-7 Outlaw Heavies.
- Outlaw Farmer combat stats 12. They attack travellers on the roads, then retreat to camps.
- Starving Vagrants: mobs of 1-13 in The Great Desert and Heng; combat stats 5; rob for food and money; Vagrant Camps spawn there.
- Both are regularly hunted and enslaved by Manhunters.

## Sand Ninjas, Bloodraiders and Grass Pirates
Also: desert bandits, Straw Lieutenant, blood bandits
- Sand Ninjas: cells of 3-5 or a lone Sand Ninja Jounin in Spine Canyon, The Great Desert and The Eye; combat stats 25, ranged 35; skilled highwaymen who attack passers-by they judge weak.
- Bloodraiders: squads of 3-11 in The Eye, Grey Desert and Spine Canyon; combat stats 23.
- Grass Pirates: 1 Straw Lieutenant with 5-13 Grass Pirates in Stormgap Coast, The Eye and The Outlands; combat stats 20; immediately hostile to the player; recognisable by low-quality Sleeveless Dustcoats; martial arts skill 20-40. A big threat to low-level squads.

## City Heroes (United Heroes League) and Black Dog
Also: vigilantes, hero patrol, extortion
- City Heroes patrol United Cities towns (guaranteed in Sho-Battai) and follow and harass non-human characters; if they follow you out of town they may attack.
- They are classed as bandits: attacking them in town is legal and guards help you.
- Their base is Fort Mirage in The Great Desert.
- Black Dog: squads of 4-6 Black Dog Bodyguards in The Outlands, Grey Desert, Flats Lagoon and Stormgap Coast; they demand protection money and attack if refused.

## Fogmen
Also: fog men, Deadhive, Fog Prince, Fog Deathyard
- Fogmen, where found: Fog Islands; lost groups of 1-5 in Dreg; ruins such as Tower of Fog and Deadhive Overrun.
- Groups: roaming squads of 7-21 (by far the most common); Fog Prince squads (1 Fog Prince, 3-5 Fog Heavies); rare hunting parties of 31-56.
- Combat stats: Fogman 15, Fog Heavy 30, Fog Prince 30. Weak singly, lethal in numbers.
- Behaviour: attack on sight; carry unconscious organics to the Prisoner Poles of a Fog Deathyard and eat them alive. Deathyards often sit next to each other.
- Downed Skeletons are usually left alone.
- A Fog Prince Head sells as a c.6,000 bounty item.
- Survival: do not fight; run to Mongrel, whose guards kill pursuers. A solo runner with speed in the low 20s mph makes it.
- Rescue a pole victim quickly, before limbs are eaten.

## Cannibals
Also: painted tribe, Scrawny Cannibal, cannibal raid
- Where: Cannibal Plains, Darkfinger, Northern Coast, Hidden Forest; raids reach Bast, Okran's Valley, Dreg, Purple Sands and Berserker Country.
- Groups: raids of 6-25 Cannibals (16 on average); packs of 10-35 Scrawny Cannibals.
- Combat stats: Cannibal 17 (red paint), Scrawny Cannibal 9 (green paint).
- Behaviour: attack on sight; carry unconscious organics to cages in a Cannibal Camp or village; when cages fill they beat prisoners to death and cook them.
- They do not eat Skeletons or animals but still attack them. Cannibals are immune to acid.
- They pick up characters sleeping on camp beds in the open; a carried sleeper cannot resist.
- Cannibal Hunters (patrols of 6-9 with Mountain Dogs) fight them and may heal wounded strangers.
- Cannibal Hunters start tip: strip heavy armour and sprint for the nearest pool of water.

## Shrieking Bandits
Also: shriekers
- Shrieking Bandits, where found: The Shrieking Forest, Purple Sands, north-west Cannibal Plains.
- Groups: 9-26, malnourished. Combat stats 25.
- Hostile. Some leaders can be answered with grunts and gestures in dialogue: the right choice avoids the fight, the wrong one starts it.

## Red Sabres and Swamp Ninjas
Also: swamp bandits, Water Clan
- Red Sabres: squads of 7-11 in The Swamp and South Wetlands; combat stats 20; red clothing, sabres; about 22 more at Red Sabre Hideout.
- Swamp Ninjas: 1 Swamp Ninja Chunin with 5-10 Swamp Ninja Genins; combat stats 25, ranged 30; Ninja Blades, Guardless Katanas, some Tooth Pick crossbows; base in South Wetlands.
- The two gangs fight each other constantly and both assault Shark.
- Swamp Ninja Masks give 100% gas and dust-storm protection, which makes their bodies worth looting.

## Reavers
Also: reaver patrol
- Reavers, where found: The Outlands (very common), Stobe's Garden, Greenbeach, The Pits East, The Crags; often seen near Brink.
- Groups: 1 Reaver Ironclad, 4-7 Reaver Bloodeds, 1-6 Reaver Slaves. Reaver Blooded combat stats 40.
- Behaviour: attack on sight; capture the defeated as forced conscripts.
- They fight Crab Raiders, Skeleton Bandits and Skin Bandits.
- Keep encumbrance low in their territory so you can run.
- Their base is Ark in Stobe's Garden.

## Skeleton Bandits, Crab Raiders and Scavengers
Also: pit gangs, south-east bandits
- Skeleton Bandits: humans in Skeleton Masks; patrols of 1 Upgraded Skeleton Bandit and 10-15 Skeleton Bandits; combat stats 43; The Crags, The Pits, Stobe's Garden, Stobe's Gamble.
- Crab Raiders: patrols of 8-14 with 4-10 Crabs; combat stats 50; strong Crab Armour; The Pits East, Greenbeach, The Pits, Stobe's Garden. They attack neutral players in the wild, though Crab Town itself admits visitors.
- Scavengers: squads of 3-20 across the south-east and Stormgap Coast; weak; attack and loot the unconscious and dead.
- Drifter "Treasure Hunters" (3-13) in The Crags and The Pits come in a friendly and an aggressive version.

## Skin Bandits
Also: skinned ones, Peeler Machine
- Skin Bandits, where found: Sonorous Dark; Horrible Ruin in Cheaters Run; they spread to The Crags and The Pits if the Skeleton Bandit leader Elder is removed.
- Groups: patrols of 8-20. Combat stats 50.
- They are Skeletons that hunt humans for skin: they knock out organics and carry them to a Peeler Machine, which removes limbs and eventually kills.
- Skeletons, Shek and Hivers are not attacked on sight at Skinhouse HQ.
- Never leave a downed organic squadmate; pick them up immediately.

## Fishmen (Gurglers)
Also: fishman patrol, Alpha Gurgler, King Gurgler
- Fishmen (Gurglers), where found: Fishman Island; patrols also reach Cheaters Run, The Pits and The Pits East.
- Groups: 20-40 Gurglers with 0-3 Alpha Gurglers.
- Combat stats 15; they fight unarmed with claws and win by numbers.
- Carry injured organics to their nests, tie them to a pole and eat them alive.
- They stop spawning once King Gurgler (in the Island Lab) is killed.
- Drops: 4 Foul Raw Meat each.

## Weak Thralls and Black Desert Ninjas
Also: Thrall Masters, Cloud Ninjas, thralls
- Weak Thralls: reprogrammed Skeletons of the Thrall Masters; squads of 2-22 in Venge (the main danger there besides the beams), sometimes The Eye and Grey Desert; combat stats 15.
- Black Desert Ninjas: squads of 3-9 Cloud Ninjas in The Black Desert, Grey Desert, Iron Valleys and around the Deadlands; combat stats 30.
- Cloud Ninjas are human and have no acid protection, so many die to the weather.

## Southern Hive, Gorillo Bandits and other southern threats
Also: Droneguard, south hive patrol
- Southern Hive: patrols of 1 Prince with 8 Droneguards, or 4 Droneguard scouts, in Royal Valley, Greyshelf and Stobe's Gamble; Droneguard combat stats 40; hostile to all outsiders.
- Gorillo Bandits: squads of 4-9 martial artists in the Bonefields and Flats Lagoon; combat stats 30; weak against ranged attacks.
- Black Dragon Ninjas: squads of 9-11 in Stobe's Garden (rare); otherwise at Tower Of Ninjas in the Border Zone.

## Sniperbots, Broken Skeletons and the Skeleton Legion
Also: Old Machines, robot sniper, Legion
- Sniperbot: spawns alone in Sniper Valley, Sonorous Dark, Stobe's Gamble, The Crags and The Pits; ranged skill 80, stealth 70; hard to spot and nearly always hits.
- Against Sniperbots: do not run in straight lines or stand idle; wear heavy armour.
- Broken Skeleton: wanders alone in Obedience, Floodlands and Iron Valleys and inside Ancient Tech Labs and Deadland Workshops; combat stats 50; hostile.
- Skeleton Legion: Ashlands and Sonorous Dark; forces of 7-22 Legions with 2-7 Legion Scouts, thrall units of 18-35; Legion combat stats 50.

## Friendly and neutral roamers
Also: who is safe to pass, harmless squads, neutral patrols, friendly caravans
- Nomads: neutral herders; Merchant Nomads caravans (1 Nomad Animal Trader, 8-10 Nomads, pack animals) sell animals. Their camps are free to use and usually have shelter, a campfire and a Camp Bed.
- Tech Hunters and Drifters: wander alone or in squads of 3-7; neutral, except some groups in Shun, The Crags, The Pits and Forbidden Isle.
- Mercenary Guild squads can be hired as bodyguards or outpost guards (the wiki's pages disagree on price; see "Mercenary Guild" in Factions); competent fighters with poor gear.
- Cannibal Hunters may heal wounded strangers.
- Trade caravans: Traders Guild (1 boss, 7-11 guards, 1-3 Pack Beasts), Western Hive, Holy Nation.
- Lone Wandering Assassins (Shinobi Thieves) heal members of the Thieves Guild.

## Acid rain
Also: acid, rain damage, burning rain
- Effect: unprotected organic body parts slowly take stun damage while it rains.
- Constant in the Deadlands and Iron Valleys; common (about 50% of the time) in Cheaters Run, Forbidden Isle and Sonorous Dark; about 25% in Burning Forest; occasional in many coastal and south-eastern regions (list in world-regions.md).
- Immune: Hivers, Skeletons and all animals.
- 80% protection is enough to heal faster than the damage; characters keep complaining aloud until protection is 100%.
- Entering any building gives full protection.
- A tent or shelter built from the build menu gives 100%: Flat Shelter, Mercenary Tent, Scrap Tent.
- Toughness does not reduce weather damage.
- Rain collectors catch no water from acid rain.
- Just after loading a save the game may briefly fail to register that a character is indoors.

## Acid rain protection gear
Also: acid rain coat, rain protection, Dustcoat, Longcoat, what to wear in acid rain
- Body armour: Dustcoat 80%, Longcoat 70%, Sleeveless Dustcoat 60%, Sleeveless Longcoat 60%, Crab Armour 50%, Plate Jacket (also White and Black) 40%.
- Headgear: Crab Helmet 60%, Ashland Hat 50%, Sandogasa 40%, Straw Hat 40%, Iron Hat 35%, Armoured Hood 25%, Rattan Hat 20%, Tricorn Hat 20%, Karuta Zukin 20%, Kusari Zukin 20%.
- Legwear: Drifters Leather Pants 10%, Plated Drifter's Leather Pants 10%.
- The values of all worn pieces add together (wiki). Aim for 80% or more: at 80% a character regenerates faster than the rain hurts, though they complain aloud until 100%.
- Rain protection does nothing against acidic ground or acidic water.

## Acidic ground
Also: acid ground, burning feet, need boots
- Deadlands, Iron Valleys and The Black Desert have acidic ground that burns the legs of characters with bare feet.
- Fix: wear any shoes or boots.
- Iron Valleys ground is 50% acidic, so it hurts less than the Deadlands.
- Hivers, Skeletons and animals are immune.
- Barefoot characters complain about their feet cooking and ask to leave.

## Acidic water and swimming
Also: acid lake, swim, drowning, swim speed, water escape
- Characters cannot drown and can swim indefinitely.
- Swim speed is 1.3 mph at Swimming 0 and rises with the skill; humans and Hivers reach 9.8 mph at 100, Shek only 5.6 mph. Skeletons walk on the bottom at a fixed 6.7 mph.
- Robot limbs reduce swim speed.
- Acidic water damages anyone who is not a Hiver or Skeleton, and armour's weather protection does not apply: Deadlands lakes, the Vain river, and water in Iron Valleys, The Black Desert, Sniper Valley, Sonorous Dark, Stobe's Gamble, The Pits, The Pits East and The Unwanted Zone.
- Animals can attack characters in shallow water; Blood Spiders and Skin Spiders walk through water.
- Safe places to train Swimming: Floodlands, Wend, South Wetlands, The Swamp, and most coasts.
- Carrying someone while swimming doubles Swimming experience.

## Poison gas
Also: gas clouds, toxic gas, my lungs
- Poison gas, where found: The Black Desert (small black clouds) and the Ashlands (large white clouds). Clouds drift across the ground.
- Effect: stun damage to the chest, rarely the stomach.
- Black Desert clouds take an unprotected Greenlander from 100 to 0 in about 14 game minutes; Ashlands clouds take about 20.
- Full protection (100%): Fog Mask, Mask Type III, Swamp Ninja Mask. Swamp Ninja Rags give 20%.
- Skeletons and animals are immune.
- Being on the top floor of a building or inside one avoids gas.
- Characters cough and say "my lungs..." in a cloud; walk out of it at once.
- No gas forms inside Cat-Lon's Exile.

## Burning beams in Venge
Also: lasers, laser, orbital beam, Venge beams, burning
- Venge only, daytime only: large moving rays appear at random, sweep quickly and last a few game minutes.
- Effect: heavy, continuous cutting damage to all organic body parts; bandage immediately afterwards.
- A beam may still be active for a moment after it stops being visible.
- Protection: Dustcoat 80%, Crab Helmet 60%, Sleeveless Dustcoat 60%, Crab Armour 50%, Armoured Hood 25%.
- Skeletons and animals are immune.
- Inside a building, including its top floor, characters take no beam damage.
- Travel at night: enter around 23:00 and be under cover by 05:00.
- Nearest shelters: Smugglers Bar (west, in Shem) and the Grey Desert Waystation (north).

## Dust storms
Also: sandstorm, duststorm, accuracy penalty, storm goggles
- Dust storms do no damage. Unprotected characters lose up to 10 points of Melee Attack and Martial Arts and up to 33.5% of Perception and Precision Shooting; weaker storms cost less.
- Vision and hearing ranges shrink for everyone, which makes storms a good time for sneaking and stealing.
- How often: The Great Desert about 78% of the time, Skinner's Roam 76%, Heng, Skimsands and Spine Canyon 68%, Spider Plains 33%, Shun and The Hook 27%, Border Zone and Bast 20%, Stenn Desert 15%; Purple Sands constantly.
- 100% protection headgear: Ashlander Stormgoggles, Square Goggles, Fog Mask, Mask Type III, Samurai Helmet, Swamp Ninja Mask.
- Partial: Basket Hat 75%, Police Helmet 75%, Tagelmust, Turban and chainmail tagelmusts 70%, Crab Helmet 60%, Ashland Hat 50%, Ninja Mask 40%, Armoured Hood 25%.
- Unprotected characters raise an arm over their eyes; that animation does not reduce the penalty.

## Nests and camps on the map
Also: paw print icon, nest, camp, spawn, clear a nest
- Nests are temporary animal lairs marked with a paw print: Wolf Den, Boneyard Wolf Den, Nest of Beak Things, River Nest, Swamp Raptor Nest, Skimmer Nest, Small Skimmer Nest, Crab Nest, Gurgler Nest, Fog Deathyard.
- Camps are their humanoid equivalent: Dust Bandit Camp, Cannibal Camp, Raider Camp, Vagrant Camp, Blood Bandit Camp, Goat Herder's Camp, Mercenary Camp, Bounty Hunter Camp, Cannibal Hunter camp.
- Camps usually have free Camp Beds. Each region allows a maximum number of nests and camps at once (13 in the Border Zone).
- Wiping out a nest's population removes it after a while and a replacement spawns somewhere else.
- A nest sends out new squads only while its area is loaded, and not while a player character stands within about 125 m of it (meitouclient notes).
- Nests may hold loot: Animal Skins, weapons, Beak Thing Eggs, Crab Eggs.

## Sleeping outdoors safely
Also: camp bed danger, kidnapped while sleeping, tent
- In Cannibal country and around Mongrel, sleeping characters can be picked up and carried away; a carried character cannot resist, so someone must stay awake to free them.
- Do not sleep on camp beds in open ground without guards in Cannibal Plains.
- Acid-rain regions: build a tent (Flat Shelter, Mercenary Tent or Scrap Tent) before resting.
- Free beds outside towns: Nomad camps, Dust Bandit Camps once cleared, Berserker Village camp beds, Crab Village beds, Smugglers Bar (upstairs), Flotsam Safehouse (2 beds), the intact shack in The Hub (2 beds).
- A Camp Bed heals at 4x, a real bed at 8x; sleeping cuts hunger rate to one third.

## Border Zone threat summary for a new squad
Also: early game dangers, first enemies, Border Zone enemies
- Hungry Bandits (combat stats 5, in mobs of 5-16): take your food if they win. Keep food in a backpack inside the inventory, or drop it before going down.
- Bandit patrols deliberately walk up to town gates, stopping about 20 m outside (meitouclient notes); The Hub's gates are ruins and do not stop them.
- Dust Bandits (15; boss 25; squads of 6-15): pay the Dust Boss or run to guards.
- Slavemongers (25; 1-8): avoid lying unconscious near them.
- Escaped Servants (15; 1-8): take food, butcher downed pets.
- Bonedogs (20): territorial; adults reach 21 mph.
- Goats and Garru: leave them alone.
- Fighters who engage bandits: Squin's Shek guards, the Waystation, Rebel Base's Ninja Guards, the Shinobi Thieves tower in The Hub (for guild members).
- West of the zone (Vain) Beak Things and Gorillos begin; south (The Swamp) Blood Spiders begin.

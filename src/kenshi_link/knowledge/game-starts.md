# Game starts

What each of Kenshi's 13 new-game starts gives you (characters, cats, gear, relations, bounties), where it puts you, how hard it is, and a sensible first hour for each.

## Choosing a start: what the new-game list shows
Also: new game, beginnings, scenario, start list, difficulty label, play style
- The base game has 13 starts: Wanderer, The Wandering Trader, The Holy Sword, The Cannibal Hunters, The Hive Exile, Son of a Captain, The Slaves, Rock Bottom, Empire Citizen, Holy Nation Citizen, Guy with a Dog, The Freedom Seekers, Nobodies.
- Each entry shows Difficulty, Cash, Play Style and a short description.
- Difficulty only describes the starting circumstances. It does not change how fast characters level or how strong enemies are.
- Play Style (RPG, Action RPG, Real-Time Strategy, Wanted Criminal, Trading RPG) is a suggestion, not a rule.
- Starts flagged malnourished begin every character at 170/300 hunger.
- Three starts restrict race: The Hive Exile (hive only), The Slaves (no Skeleton), Holy Nation Citizen (Human only).
- "Hungover Ninja" exists only in the game files and cannot be picked.

## Which start to pick for a first game
Also: best start, beginner start, recommended start, easiest
- Wanderer is described in game as "the way the game is intended to be played": one character, c.1,000, in The Hub, default relations, no bounty.
- Nobodies gives five bodies for free, which is the strongest safety net (someone is always left to bandage and carry), but they start hungry with no cats.
- The Freedom Seekers gives six characters, c.4,000 and building supplies, but spawns at a random Waystation, so the surroundings vary.
- The Wandering Trader starts with a pack animal and stock but no cats, next to the United Cities town Heng.
- Avoid for a first game: The Cannibal Hunters (attacked immediately), Rock Bottom (one arm, starving, alone), The Slaves (start imprisoned), The Hive Exile (dangerous zone, cannot trade with the hive).
- For an agent playing through screenshots, a start in The Hub keeps copper, a bar and cheap housing within a short walk.

## Wanderer (start)
Also: default start, lone wanderer, 1000 cats start
- Location: The Hub (Border Zone).
- Cats: c.1,000. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in every stat.
- Gear: an Iron Club of Unknown quality and a Rag Loincloth. (The in-game blurb says "rusty sword", but the wiki lists an Iron Club.)
- Not malnourished. No race limit.
- Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Default.
- An Unknown-quality Iron Club is nearly worthless: value about c.180 to c.198, sell value about c.45 to c.49.

## Wanderer: first hour
Also: wanderer first steps, what to do first, early plan
- Walk to the bar in The Hub (a Black Market Bar, open day and night). Buy Dried Meat (about c.78 each, 15 nutrition) and a Basic First Aid Kit (about c.76). Keep most of the c.1,000.
- Copper and iron nodes are within walking distance of The Hub's gates. Mine Copper (base c.180 each), carry it back, sell it at the bar.
- A forum post places two copper nodes downhill from the gate nearest the bar (unverified)
- Do not fight. Dust Bandits and Starving Bandits roam the Border Zone; the bar's Ninja Guards attack bandits that come near the bar.
- Targets, in order: a Wooden Backpack (c.800, sold at Travel Gear shops such as in Squin), a collapsed Small Shack in The Hub (c.1,500), a second character (generic Recruit c.3,000).
- Hobbs, a free recruit, sometimes spawns in The Hub's bar: act interested in his stories.

## The Wandering Trader (start)
Also: trader start, pack bull start
- Location: Trader's Edge, a Traders Guild outpost joined to the United Cities town Heng by a metal bridge.
- Cats: c.0. Relations: +75 with the Traders Guild. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in all stats, plus a teen Pack Bull with about 25 in combat stats.
- Worn: Trader's Wooden Backpack, Wooden Sandals, Trader's Leathers (Shoddy, 5% chance Standard), Samurai Clothpants. The bull wears a Bull Backpack.
- Inventory: 5 Luxury Goods, 6 Hacksaws, 2 Gohan, 2 Standard First Aid Kits.
- Not malnourished. Play style: Trading RPG. Difficulty: Easy.

## The Wandering Trader: first hour
Also: trader first steps, sell starting goods
- You have stock but no cats. Luxury Goods have a base value of c.240 and Hacksaws c.72; each town prices trade goods within about 30% of base, so compare the price shown in Trader's Edge and in Heng before selling.
- Keep the 2 Gohan (75 nutrition each) as food; the bull eats from the shared packs too.
- With default settings the free recruit Riddly is in one of Heng's two bars. Tell her you want her along on an adventure, not that you are building a settlement.
- Then trade: buy whichever trade goods show the lowest markup, carry them far (prices barely differ between neighbouring towns) and sell where the markup is high.
- The Bull Backpack (20x20 grid, stacks x5, 90% weight reduction) should carry the bulk goods.
- Slavers and Manhunters operate around United Cities land: do not get knocked out alone.

## The Holy Sword (start)
Also: wanted criminal start, legendary sword start, 20000 bounty start
- Location: The Hub. Cats: c.100.
- Relations: -100 with The Holy Nation (and with "Imperial Lords", a faction the game does not use).
- Bounty: c.20,000, wanted by The Holy Nation and United Cities. Only this first character carries it.
- Squad: one character with stats around 1 (randomised by 3).
- Gear: an Edgewalkers Nodachi (equal chance of Edge Type 1, 2 or 3), Black Cloth Shirt, Ninja Pants, Ninja Rags (Shoddy, 5% chance Standard), one Standard First Aid Kit.
- Play style: Wanted Criminal. Difficulty: Harder.
- The wiki states United Cities guards do not attack this character for the bounty, because the hostile relation is with the unused Imperial Lords.

## The Holy Sword: first hour
Also: holy sword first steps, sell the holy sword
- The Nodachi is worth far more than everything else you own: value about c.45,600 to c.49,700, sell value about c.11,400 to c.12,400 (weapons sell at 25% of value).
- It needs Strength 8 and weighs 8 kg, and it is 1x10 in the grid, so it only fits a weapon slot or a backpack.
- Option A: keep it and train with it. Option B: sell it for an immediate bankroll; one small shop may not hold that many cats, so check the trader's money first.
- Stay out of Holy Nation towns (Stack, Bad Teeth, Blister Hill) and away from Holy Nation patrols: -100 relations plus a c.20,000 bounty.
- The Hub is run by Holy Nation Outlaws, not The Holy Nation, so it is a usable base.
- A bounty above c.10,000 never expires by itself.
- Otherwise follow the Wanderer plan: food, copper, backpack, shack.

## The Cannibal Hunters (start)
Also: cannibal start, attacked immediately
- Location: a random Cannibal Village (they stand in the Cannibal Plains and Darkfinger). Cats: c.9. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: two characters. The first has combat skills of 5 and carries a Standard First Aid Kit. The second has 2 in combat skills, 20 in thievery skills, a Standard First Aid Kit and a Splint Kit.
- Not malnourished. Play style: Action RPG. Difficulty: Dodgy.
- The start screen warns that you are attacked the moment the game begins and that it is not for a first play.
- A village is garrisoned by several squads: groups of 2 Cannibal Chiefs with 9 to 13 Cannibals, and groups of 10 to 35 Scrawny Cannibals.

## The Cannibal Hunters: first hour
Also: cannibal hunters first steps, escape at the start
- Keep the game paused on load and give run orders before time moves.
- Do not fight: being captured by cannibals normally ends in death.
- Take off anything that lowers Athletics or over-encumbers you, set both characters to run, and sprint out of the village.
- The wiki's advice is to head for the nearest pool of water.
- Keep the two characters together so one can bandage or carry the other.
- Once clear, treat wounds with the Standard First Aid Kits, then travel to a town before trying to earn anything; you have c.9.
- Which town is nearest depends on which village you spawned in: open the map before choosing a direction.

## The Hive Exile (start)
Also: hive start, hiveless start, hiver start
- Location: a Hive Village (Western Hive land; villages are in Vain and Dreg). Cats: c.0. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in all stats.
- Race limit: a Western Hive Prince, Soldier Drone or Worker Drone.
- Not malnourished. Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Hard.
- As a Hiveless you cannot buy from or sell to the traders in Hive Villages, and they refuse your whole squad while a hiveless character is in it.
- Roaming Hive Caravans do trade with a Hiveless.
- Vain has Beak Things and Gorillos; villages are often attacked by them.

## The Hive Exile: first hour
Also: hive exile first steps, hive exile survival
- You have no first aid kit, so one bad wound can kill. The wiki's main advice is to leave Vain quickly and get first aid kits.
- Vain borders the Border Zone to the east; The Hub and Squin are the Border Zone's towns.
- Hive characters cannot wear boots and only wear hive-variant shirts; hive Workers and Princes drain hunger at 0.5x, Soldiers at 0.6x.
- Hive Soldier Drones and Worker Drones can eat Foul Raw Meat; all hivers can eat Raw Meat.
- A risky alternative from the wiki: steal Beak Thing Eggs (c.4,200 each) from a nest at night and sell them to a Hive Caravan.
- If chased by animals, run into a Hive Village: the soldier drones only join in once the animal hits a drone.

## Son of a Captain (start)
Also: captain's son, retired captain, katana start
- Location: The Hub. Cats: c.0.
- Relations: +100 with the United Cities, -100 with The Holy Nation. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in all stats.
- Gear: Leather Shirt (Shoddy, 5% chance Standard), Samurai Clothpants, Drifter's Boots, a Catun Scrapmaster Katana (equal chance of Catun No.1, 2 or 3), some Gohan, one Standard First Aid Kit.
- Not malnourished. Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Normal.
- The Katana's value is about c.9,400 to c.14,400 depending on grade; it sells for about c.2,400 to c.3,600.

## Son of a Captain: first hour
Also: captain first steps, captain early plan
- You have food (Gohan, 75 nutrition each) and a good sword but no cats.
- The Holy Nation attacks you on sight. Stack, Bad Teeth, Blister Hill and Holy Nation patrols are off limits; Holy Nation land borders the Border Zone (Okran's Pride to the north, and Okran's Gulf).
- The United Cities are allies, but none of their towns is in the Border Zone (its only towns are The Hub and Squin).
- Earn first cats the Wanderer way: mine Copper near The Hub and sell it at the bar.
- Wiki tip: the Standard First Aid Kit starts with a random number of charges; selling it to a shop, closing the window, reopening and buying it back returns it full (you pay for the refill).
- Keep the Katana: Catun grades are far better than anything a new squad can buy early.

## The Slaves (start)
Also: slave start, start in shackles
- Location: Rebirth, the Holy Nation labour camp north of its main territory. Cats: c.0. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: 2 characters (the number scales with the Squad Size Multiplier setting) with 1 in all stats.
- Gear: Dyed Trousers, Dyed Robes and locked Prisoner Shackles.
- Malnourished (170/300 hunger). No Skeleton characters.
- Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Hard.
- The start's own hint: to escape a beating, step into a cage at once.

## The Slaves: first hour (escaping Rebirth)
Also: escape Rebirth, slave escape, lockpick shackles
- Pause on load, then walk both characters into a cage as the hint says.
- Let them work as slaves until night.
- At night, after being put in a cage or on a pole, pick the locks (cage and shackles), switch on sneak, and optionally slip into a building for food.
- Leave Rebirth heading east. The south and west exits can be covered by guards on Mounted Crossbows.
- Freeing the slaves on poles outside gives the guards other targets.
- Unlocking shackles or freeing prisoners counts as Terrorism (c.5,000 bounty) and escaping prison adds c.2,500 if you are seen.
- After escaping, the Flotsam Safehouse north of Rebirth (shows as "Lone Shack" from afar) has food, low-level gear and 2 beds; reach it by looping around the mountains or swimming through Wend.
- Time spent caged is good Lockpicking and Stealth training.

## Rock Bottom (start)
Also: one arm start, missing arm, very hard start, desert start
- Location: The Great Desert, east of Sho-Battai. Cats: c.0. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in all stats, missing the left arm, unarmed and naked.
- Malnourished (170/300 hunger). No race limit.
- Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Very Hard.
- Being starving is against the law in United Cities territory: at a town gate a Gate Sergeant may question you and, depending on personality, attack.
- A Robot Limb can later go in the empty limb slot.

## Rock Bottom: first hour
Also: rock bottom survival, rock bottom first steps
- Do not walk straight into Sho-Battai while starving (see the gate risk above).
- Wiki route: go east to the Old Village, a ruin with two working wells and loose low-level loot (Fuel, Iron Plates, Steel Bars, Building Materials, Fabrics, Sleeping Bags, sometimes Advanced Splint Kits and Authentic Skeleton Repair Kits). Take what sells.
- For food, trail wild Garru, Skimmers or Rebel Farmers with a Bonedog until something is knocked out, take the Raw Meat and cook it on a Campfire (buildable anywhere, no materials) into Dried Meat.
- Sell the loot and buy food from a Hive Caravan in the desert or at the Distant Hive Villages to the north-west.
- Slavers and Manhunters patrol this desert; being knocked out here usually means enslavement.
- Skimmers also roam this desert: keep well clear of them.

## Empire Citizen (start)
Also: citizen start, 750 cats start
- Location: Sho-Battai, a United Cities town in The Great Desert. Cats: c.750. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one character with 1 in all stats.
- Gear: Rag Shirt, Halfpants (ragged), Wooden Sandals.
- Not malnourished. Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Default.
- Sho-Battai has two bars, a bakery, General Shop, Mechanical Shop, Ranger Shop, Headgear Shop, Travel Gear, Slave Shop, Police Station and a Thieves Guild tower.
- Houses for sale there: Small Shack x2 (c.4,000 each), Longhouse x3 (c.19,200 each).

## Empire Citizen: first hour
Also: empire citizen first steps, citizen early plan
- Buy food first; in United Cities land starving is a crime.
- A United Heroes League squad always spawns at a Sho-Battai bar or food shop. The game classes them as bandits, so attacking them in town makes the guards help you. Their gear (the Hero Boss carries an Unknown-quality Nodachi) sells in any town without trouble. Let the guards do the fighting.
- Sand Ninja Jounin patrolling near town carry a bounty of c.6,000 to c.10,000; lure one to the gate guards and hand it in at the Police Station.
- Riddly, a free recruit, can appear in a Sho-Battai bar or bakery.
- Skimmers, slavers and Manhunters roam the surrounding desert: stay near the gates until you have help.
- An outpost built within 3,500 m of a United Cities town draws a tax visit demanding c.3,000 a week, so do not found one here early.

## Holy Nation Citizen (start)
Also: Holy Nation start, Okranite start
- Location: Bad Teeth or Stack (Holy Nation towns). Cats: c.200.
- Relations: +15 with The Holy Nation. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one Human character with 1 in all stats.
- Gear: Iron Club (Unknown quality), Stout Hessian and Drifter's Boots (each Shoddy, 5% chance Standard), Cloth Shirt, and one copy of The Holy Flame in the inventory.
- Not malnourished. Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Default.
- Keep The Holy Flame: carrying it gets you through many encounters with Paladins and Inquisitors.
- Non-human squad members are attacked on sight in Holy Nation territory, and Priests question female characters.

## Holy Nation Citizen: first hour
Also: holy citizen first steps, Holy Nation start plan
- If a Priest questions a female character, do not insult him: that leads to arrest and Rebirth.
- Never try to bribe Holy Nation guards and never mention Narko to them.
- Starting in Stack: the wiki suggests selling the low-grade armour at the Boot Shop and buying Wooden Sandals so you can outrun hostiles.
- Stack has a General Shop, Mechanical Shop, bars, bakery and cheap housing: Small Shack x2 collapsed (c.1,500) and x2 intact (c.4,000). Starving Bandits, Bonedogs and Dust Bandits roam outside.
- Starting in Bad Teeth: gate guards often kill River Raptors, which can be looted for profit. Housing: Small Shack x2 (c.4,000), collapsed Longhouse (c.7,200).
- Recruiting non-humans later makes Holy Nation land dangerous for the squad.

## Guy with a Dog (start)
Also: dog start, puppy start, 13 cats start
- Location: Bast, a ruined United Cities city where United Cities and Holy Nation patrols fight.
- Cats: c.13. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: one humanoid with 1 in all stats, plus a baby Bonedog with about 20 in combat stats (shown near zero until it ages).
- Gear: Basic First Aid Kit, Rag Loincloth, Iron Stick (Unknown quality).
- Malnourished (170/300 hunger). Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Normal.
- The puppy is very slow at first; carrying it is often faster.

## Guy with a Dog: first hour
Also: dog start first steps, feed the dog
- Bast's large United Cities and Holy Nation patrols fight whenever they meet. Wait until one whole side is down, then loot the losers.
- Loot Holy Nation gear: the nearest places to sell (Drin's bar, then Stoat or Sho-Battai) are United Cities.
- Do not wear looted faction uniforms.
- Drin, north-east in Bast, has a bar with cheap Dried Meat and free beds. Enter from the sides, not through the gate.
- Odd wiki fact: putting your character and dog into the cages in Bast refills their hunger up to 200.
- Bonedog nests are nearby and are regularly attacked by the patrols; Skimmers roam the desert toward Stoat and Sho-Battai.
- Cannibal patrols can also spawn in the area.
- Bast's abandoned cages are a safe place to train Lockpicking.

## The Freedom Seekers (start)
Also: base building start, six characters start, RTS start
- Location: one of the four Tech Hunter Waystations, chosen at random (Border Zone, Grey Desert, Stenn Desert, The Hook).
- Cats: c.4,000. Relations: default.
- Squad: 6 characters. Five have 1 in all stats, Halfpants (ragged) and Unknown-quality Wakizashis. The sixth, an "Old Soldier", has stats around 20 to 25, an Ancient Horse Chopper, Leather Shirt, Samurai Clothpants and Drifter's Boots.
- Bounty: the Old Soldier is wanted by The Holy Nation for c.20,000.
- Shared supplies: 5 Wooden Backpacks, 20 Building Materials, 20 Iron Plates, 4 Fabrics, 10 Wheatstraw, 17 Gohan, 9 Basic First Aid Kits, 1 Standard First Aid Kit, and a Wheatstraw Farming blueprint.
- Storm House and Storage: Wheatstraw are already researched.
- Not malnourished. Play style: Real-Time Strategy. Difficulty: Easy / Hard combination.

## The Freedom Seekers: first hour
Also: freedom seekers plan, freedom seekers first steps
- Open the map first to learn which Waystation you are at; the right plan depends on the zone.
- Do not found an outpost immediately: any outpost draws raids, and five of six characters cannot fight.
- Waystations have an Adventurer Bar (food, first aid kits, open all night) and an Adventurer Mechanical Shop (Building Materials, Iron Plates, Books).
- Use the 17 Gohan as travel food and keep the first aid kits spread across characters.
- Safer opening: walk to a town with cheap housing, buy a building, and use the Building Materials and Iron Plates for a research bench and storage.
- Put the miners to work in pairs on a copper node while the Old Soldier stands guard.
- Per the wiki nobody acts on the Old Soldier's bounty; a squadmate can hand him in once for c.20,000 and then break him out.

## Nobodies (start)
Also: five characters start, squad start, nobodies
- Location: The Hub, or one of the two Waystation Ruins (in Venge or in Stobe's Garden).
- Cats: c.0. Relations: default. Bounty: none.
- Squad: 5 characters with 1 in all stats.
- Gear: each has an Iron Stick (Unknown quality) and Cargopants, plus 2 Basic First Aid Kits in their inventory.
- Malnourished (170/300 hunger). Play style: RPG. Difficulty: Easy.
- Another guide says the possible spawns are the Hub, Stobe's Garden or Venge, which matches the wiki.

## Nobodies: first hour
Also: nobodies plan, nobodies first steps, five miners
- Five hungry people and no cats: food is the first problem.
- In The Hub: put all five on copper (a node takes 1 to 3 workers depending on its size, so use several nodes), sell at the bar, buy Dried Meat.
- At the Waystation Ruins in Venge: loot the two Skeleton Repair Kits (c.4,341 each) and the other loose items, then go west to the Smugglers Bar to sell them for food, or (riskier) north-east to the Grey Desert Waystation.
- At the Waystation Ruins in Stobe's Garden: loot the same way (two Skeleton Repair Kits, two Skeleton Eyes at c.3,600, a Power Core), then go straight north to a pool of water and from there north-west to Black Scratch. Do not go west: the Unwanted Zone is full of Beak Thing nests.
- Keep one character out of every fight to bandage the rest.

## Starting in The Hub: what the town offers
Also: Hub start, Hub facilities, starting town
- The Hub is a mostly ruined town in the middle of the Border Zone, nominally held by Holy Nation Outlaws. It has no major-faction police.
- Bar: a Black Market Bar run by the Trade Ninjas. Open day and night, restocks every 24 hours, sells food, Basic First Aid Kits, building supplies and some weapons, and buys stolen goods (except goods stolen from itself).
- Thieves Guild tower of the Shinobi Thieves at the far end of town: joining costs c.10,000.
- One intact Small Shack (not for sale) has two beds; some occupants may attack if they see you inside.
- Every house for sale is collapsed and needs Building Materials to repair; the cheapest is a Small Shack at c.1,500. The full list is under "The Hub: houses for sale" in Towns.

## Malnourished starts: getting food fast
Also: hungry start, starving at the start, 170 hunger, first food
- The Slaves, Rock Bottom, Guy with a Dog and Nobodies start at 170/300 hunger.
- Below 200 hunger, movement and combat are noticeably slowed.
- Characters eat by themselves when hunger drops below 250 if any squad inventory or backpack holds food.
- Cheapest bought food per point: Dried Meat (c.78, 15 nutrition) and Gohan (c.444, 75 nutrition).
- Free food: Raw Meat from dead animals, cooked into Dried Meat at a Campfire, which can be placed anywhere at no material cost.
- Only hivers, Scorchlanders and animals can eat Raw Meat uncooked.
- Skeleton characters never need food (hunger rate 0).

## Starting race: what it changes early
Also: race choice, which race to start as, starting as a non-human
- Greenlander: bonus XP in Cooking, Farming, Science; no penalties; hunger rate 1x.
- Scorchlander: bonus in Athletics, Dodge, Stealth, smithing; penalty in Labouring, Farming, Cooking; hunger rate 0.9x.
- Shek: bonus in Attack, Strength, Toughness; 125 health per body part; hunger rate 1.25x; penalties in Labouring, Athletics, Stealth, Science.
- Hive Worker: bonus in Labouring, Athletics, Farming, Stealth, Thievery; low health; hunger rate 0.5x.
- Skeleton: 200 health per part, no food needed, but heals only with Skeleton Repair Kits or a Skeleton Repair Bed, and cannot wear boots, shirts or helmets.
- Only humans (Greenlander, Scorchlander) can enter Holy Nation territory without being attacked on sight.
- XP bonuses and penalties are +20% and -20% unless noted.

## Bounties and hostile factions you start with
Also: starting bounty, wanted at start, starting relations by game start, default relations, who hates me at the start
- The Holy Sword: c.20,000 bounty (The Holy Nation, United Cities) on the starting character; -100 relations with The Holy Nation.
- Son of a Captain: -100 with The Holy Nation, +100 with the United Cities.
- The Freedom Seekers: the Old Soldier has a c.20,000 Holy Nation bounty; the others have none.
- The Wandering Trader: +75 with the Traders Guild.
- Holy Nation Citizen: +15 with The Holy Nation.
- All other starts: default relations, no bounty.
- A bounty over c.10,000 is "notorious" and does not expire with time. Up to c.25,000 it can still be cleared by serving the jail sentence; bail costs twice the bounty and, per game-code notes, is offered only by Shek and Holy Nation officers.
- Bandit factions (Dust Bandits, Starving Bandits, cannibals) are hostile in every start.

## First recruits: cost and where to find them
Also: second character, first companion, early recruit cost
- Recruits for hire stand in bars or walk around towns. Talking to them and paying adds them to the squad at once.
- Typical fee: c.3,000 to c.7,500. A generic "Recruit" costs c.3,000.
- Free unique recruits include Hobbs (may be in The Hub, Mourn, Waystations or World's End) and Riddly (bars in United Cities towns).
- Bar recruits do not respawn once hired or killed.
- Squad limit: 30 characters in total.
- Mercenaries can be hired from bars as bodyguards for c.1,000 or c.2,000 per day (the wiki's pages disagree; see "Mercenary Guild" in Factions).
- A second character matters more than better gear early: one stays out of fights and bandages the other.

# Recruits

How to add characters to the squad in Kenshi: the recruiting process, squad limits, generic bar recruits and their prices, and every unique recruit with location, cost, conditions and strengths.

## How recruiting works
Also: get more characters, hire people, new squad members, companions
- New characters join through dialogue, almost always in towns.
- Recruits for hire stand in bars or wander the town; you pay a set fee and they become player characters at once.
- The fee is generally c.3,000 to c.7,500; some unique recruits join for free.
- Three kinds exist: Generic Recruits (random name, weak), Unique Recruits (named, one per game, often better stats), and freed slaves or prisoners who decide to join.
- A hired recruit usually arrives weak (very low stats), dressed in rags, armed with something simple and hungry: have food and spare gear ready.
- Recruits have no morale or loyalty to manage (unverified)
- The player faction is called Nameless until renamed in the Faction menu.

## Recruit dialogue: what can go wrong
Also: talk to recruit, hire dialogue, recruit refuses, rude answer
- Generic recruits carry ordinary random names, so you find them by talking to bar patrons who are not the barman or guards. The clicks: "Recruit at a bar" in How-to.
- A recruit states a price or a story and offers a line that hires them; the fee is taken from your cats and they join immediately.
- Without enough cats the hiring line will not go through (unverified)
- The recruit then appears among your squad portraits (unverified)
- Rude answers can cost you the recruit: Hobbs never talks to you again, Stubs Momuso ends the talk or attacks.
- Which cursor or icon marks a character that has dialogue is not described on the wiki (unverified)

## Squad size limits
Also: maximum characters, maximum squad size, squad cap, 30 limit, too many recruits, cannot recruit more
- You can control at most 30 characters.
- At 30, recruitment lines no longer appear in dialogue.
- Animals you buy count toward the 30.
- The wiki says the cap is raised with mods; the unmodded game has no setting for it.
- The game's settings define three values: max faction size, max squad size (how many can be dragged into one squad) and max squads.
- kenshi.zone, seen only through search results, gives 20 per squad and 10 squads, with a new squad opening when the last one reaches 20 (unverified)
- More characters cost more computer performance.
- Drag portraits between squad tabs to reorganise (unverified)

## Who can be recruited at all
Also: recruitable rule, why won't he join, strong characters
- Diplomats, law enforcers, military characters and traders are never recruitable through the normal routes.
- A character is also excluded if (Strength or Dexterity) + (Melee Attack or Martial Arts) + (Melee Defence or Dodge) totals 75 or more, taking the higher value of each pair.
- Freed slaves usually refuse if their Strength, Dexterity and Toughness add up to more than 75.
- So only weak or middling characters join; strong fighters must be trained.
- A few Shek with Honorable or Maniacal personality skip this check when rescued.

## Bar recruits never respawn
Also: empty bar, no more recruits, recruits ran out
- Bar squads do not respawn or change over time.
- Once you hire everyone in a bar, or they are killed, that bar stays empty for the rest of the game.
- Unique recruits appear only once per game, and their listed locations are only possible spawn points; some may not spawn at all.
- When a town is empty, go to another town.
- Protect bars from bandit fights you drag into town: dead recruits are gone.

## Freed slaves and prisoners as recruits
Also: slave recruits, rescue, unlock cage, buy a slave
- Unlocking a cage and shackles, or buying a slave from a Slaver Boss, makes the character an ally: a green dot on the map that follows whoever freed them.
- Kept safe and led far enough from their captors, they either join permanently or run off.
- Some refuse to leave their cage, depending on personality.
- Unique prisoners (Cat, Luquin, Espher, Sinklyde, Agnu) join after being freed.
- Being seen freeing slaves or prisoners is Terrorism (c.5,000 bounty).
- Rebirth and Slave Shops are the main sources; both are heavily guarded.
- A character you kidnapped or sold yourself will not join out of gratitude.

## "You saved my life!" recruits
Also: rescued NPC joins, healed stranger, lifesaver dialogue
- A character you healed or freed, then left alone, may greet you with a special dialogue when you meet again.
- It needs more than 24 in-game hours since they last spoke to you.
- You must never have kidnapped them; carrying by "pick up" is fine.
- Traitorous personalities never offer it.
- Honorable, recruitable characters can then be asked to bodyguard you or join for good.
- Ordinary civilians can be persuaded by saying the work is labour; that answer always succeeds.
- You must be in good health when you talk to them.

## Animals as squad members
Also: buy a dog, pet, pack animal, animal companions
- Animals bought from Nomads, Farm Shops or Holy Farms join the squad at once and count toward the 30-character cap.
- Weak bandit factions butcher unconscious animals for meat.
- An animal's speed depends mostly on its age.
- Cornelius the goat is the only unique animal recruit.

## Taming animals (not possible)
Also: tame, taming, tame a wild animal, capture an animal, catch an animal
- Wild animals cannot be tamed. The wiki says taming was hinted at during development, with Beak Things and Crabs the likely candidates, but it is not in the game.
- Animals join a squad only by purchase: Nomads sell Goats, Pack Beasts and others, Holy Farms sell Bonedogs and Domesticated Bulls, and the Crab Raiders sell Crabs in their towns.
- A bought animal keeps only a quarter of its Toughness, Attack, Defence, Dodge, Athletics and similar skills.
- See "Animals as squad members".

## Holy Nation errands need a human male
Also: male recruit, hand in bounties Holy Nation
- Only a human male can hand bounties to Inquisitors, bail prisoners or sell captives at Rebirth.
- An all-female or non-human squad that wants Holy Nation standing should hire a Holy Nation Male Recruit (c.3,000) in Stack, Bad Teeth or Blister Hill.
- Keep his natural limbs: robot limbs get him attacked there.
- Reva refuses to be hired by a human male.

## Agnu (unique recruit)
Also: Soldierbot recruit, strongest recruit

- He is also the one who sells the Traders Guild alliance (c.100,000, or c.75,000 with an outpost).

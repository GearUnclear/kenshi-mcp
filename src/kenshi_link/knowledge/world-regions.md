# World regions

Every named region (zone) of the Kenshi world map: where it lies, who controls it, what roams there, weather and terrain hazards, how dangerous it is for a weak new squad, and what is worth going there for.

## How region directions were worked out
Also: compass, bearings, accuracy of directions, map size
- "Neighbours" lists come from each region's wiki page; the wiki does not give their directions.
- Each compass direction here is the bearing between two regions' centre points on the Fandom interactive Location map. Regions are irregular, so even measured ones are accurate only to about one compass point.
- For 45 regions the centre is the average position of the locations the wiki maps inside them (for 14 of them a single location).
- For the other 27 regions the centre was set by hand when this file was written, not measured. Their own "Neighbours" lines are tagged (unverified), and their names carry "(est.)" in other regions' lines.
- North is up on the in-game map; west is left.
- The whole map is about 29.5 km wide: a 64 x 64 grid of zones of 4,608 game units each (meitouclient notes), at 0.1 m per unit (wiki).

## Estimated directions: what "(est.)" and the unverified tag mean
Also: estimated directions, unverified directions, est., accuracy of neighbours
- "(est.)" after a region name in a "Neighbours" line means the direction to that region is this file's estimate, not a measurement or a wiki statement.
- A whole "Neighbours" line tagged (unverified) belongs to a region whose own centre point was set by hand; every direction in it is an estimate.
- Expect an estimated direction to be right to within one or two compass points (for example "NE" may really be N or E).
- The neighbour relationship itself (which regions touch) is from the wiki in every case.
- Directions the wiki states in words, such as the Border Zone lying "south of Okran's Pride and east of Vain", are not marked.
- Before relying on an estimated direction for travel, check the in-game map.

## Regions whose centre point was set by hand (list)
Also: which regions are approximate, estimated region positions, hand-placed regions
- Re-centred by hand from markers of locations inside the region, because the automatic average was skewed by wiki pages that cover several regions at once: Vain, Cannibal Plains, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Okran's Valley, Arm of Okran, Skimsands, Skinner's Roam.
- Placed from one or two markers attributed to the region when this file was written: Obedience, The Iron Trail, Purple Sands, Spine Canyon, Greenbeach, Iron Valleys.
- No marker at all; placed from the neighbour list and the positions of neighbouring regions, guided by the wiki's written description where it has one: The Shrieking Forest, Berserker Country, The Eye, Islands of None, The Black Desert, High Bonefields, Shun, Watcher's Rim, None Coast, Greyshelf, The Unwanted Zone, Sniper Valley, Sonorous Junk Reef.

## How danger ratings and squad sizes in this file were assigned
Also: danger rating meaning, low moderate high extreme, squad size ranges
- Squad sizes such as "5-9" are the wiki's spawn-list ranges for one squad.
- "Danger for a new squad" is this file's own summary of the wiki's spawn lists: low = weak bandits and slow animals; moderate = organised bandits or patrols; high = fast predators or large mobs; extreme = things that eat, enslave or outrun almost anyone.

## World map overview: west, centre and north
Also: world layout, where is everything, map of Kenshi, quadrants
- Centre-west: Border Zone (The Hub, Squin), the usual starting region.
- A valley in the south of Skinner's Roam, just north-east of the Border Zone, is the geographic centre of the world map.
- North of the Border Zone: Holy Nation heartland (Okran's Gulf with Stack; Okran's Pride with Blister Hill and Bad Teeth; Rebirth; Wend; Okran's Valley).
- West and south-west: Vain and Dreg (Western Hive), Stenn Desert (Shek capital Admag), Spider Plains, Arach; north-west: Fog Islands (Mongrel).
- Centre: Deadlands (Black Desert City), Iron Valleys, The Black Desert, Grey Desert, Venge, Shem.
- North and far north-west: Floodlands, Hidden Forest, Cannibal Plains, Northern Coast, Darkfinger, Leviathan Coast.

## World map overview: east and south
Also: world layout, east side, south side, south-east
- North-east: The Great Desert (Heft, Stoat, Sho-Battai, Bark), Heng, Bast, Sinkuun, Skimsands. This is United Cities land.
- East coast: Stormgap Coast (Brink), The Outlands (Black Scratch), Gut, Howler Maze.
- South: The Swamp (Shark), South Wetlands, The Hook (Clownsteady, Drifter's Last), Bonefields (Catun, Mourn), Flats Lagoon.
- Far south: Royal Valley and Greyshelf (Southern Hive), Fishman Island, Cheaters Run.
- South-east: Stobe's Gamble (Spring), Stobe's Garden, The Crags, The Pits, Sonorous Dark, and the Ashlands in the corner.
- South-west corner: Shun, The Crater, The Grid.

## Safest regions for a weak new squad
Also: beginner regions, where to start, safe zones, low danger
- Border Zone: the wiki calls it the mildest region in the game; weather is not life-threatening, animals are few and slow, roaming bandits are unskilled, and beaten characters are usually left alive.
- Okran's Pride and Okran's Gulf: heavily patrolled by The Holy Nation and safe for human males; risky for non-humans, Skeletons and anyone with robot limbs, and restricted for women without a male escort.
- Stenn Desert between Squin and Admag: Shek patrols and light wildlife, though Berserkers and Kral's Chosen roam.
- Shem: flat, no tax-collecting faction; Dust Bandits and small Beak Thing groups are the risk.
- Flats Lagoon: relatively safe compared with the rest of the south-east.
- One step outside the Border Zone the threat jumps: Vain (west) has Beak Things and Gorillos, The Swamp (south) has Blood Spiders.

## Regions to avoid with a weak squad
Also: dangerous zones, death zones, where not to go, late game regions
- Fauna-heavy regions, in the wiki's rough order of rising threat: Leviathan Coast, The Unwanted Zone, Gut, Bonefields, Arach.
- Eaten alive: Fog Islands (Fogmen), Cannibal Plains, Darkfinger, Northern Coast and Hidden Forest (Cannibals), Fishman Island and Cheaters Run (Fishmen).
- Enslaved or worse when knocked out: The Outlands and Stobe's Garden (Reavers), The Hook, Heng and The Great Desert (Manhunters, Slave Hunters), Rebirth (Holy Nation slave mine), Sonorous Dark (Skin Bandits peel organic characters).
- Deadly weather or ground: Deadlands, Iron Valleys, The Black Desert (acid, gas), Venge (daytime laser beams), Ashlands (gas, Skeleton Legion).
- Fast predators a new character cannot outrun: Beak Things in Vain, Gut, Bonefields, The Crater, Shem, Venge, Stormgap Coast.
- Places that kill captured characters outright: Cannibal Capital, Horrible Ruin (Cheaters Run), Cult Village (Stormgap Coast).

## Regions with acid rain, acidic ground or acidic water
Also: acid rain zones, acid ground, acid water, where does it rain acid
- Constant acid rain: Deadlands and Iron Valleys.
- Frequent (about 50% of the time): Cheaters Run, Forbidden Isle, Sonorous Dark.
- Sometimes: Burning Forest (about 25%, in its acid season), Howler Maze and Islands of None (18%), The Crags, The Pits and The Pits East (12%), Fishman Island (8%), Royal Valley, Greyshelf and The Unwanted Zone (6%).
- Rare (about 0.3%): Northern Coast, Leviathan Coast, The Iron Trail, Cannibal Plains, Darkfinger, Dreg, Greenbeach, Stormgap Coast, Raptor Island.
- Acidic ground (burns bare feet): Deadlands, Iron Valleys, The Black Desert.
- Acidic water (do not swim): Deadlands, Iron Valleys, The Black Desert, Vain, Sniper Valley, Sonorous Dark, Stobe's Gamble, The Pits, The Pits East, The Unwanted Zone.

## Regions with poison gas, laser beams or dust storms
Also: gas zones, laser zone, sandstorm zones, duststorm regions
- Poison gas clouds: The Black Desert (small black clouds, stronger) and Ashlands (large white clouds).
- Burning laser beams: Venge only, daytime only.
- Dust storms reduce accuracy but do no damage.
- Dust storms most of the time: Purple Sands (constant), The Great Desert (78%), Skinner's Roam (76%), Heng, Skimsands and Spine Canyon (68%).
- Dust storms sometimes: Spider Plains (33%), Shun and The Hook (27%), Burning Forest and The Shrieking Forest (24%), Border Zone and Bast (20%), The Crags, The Pits and The Pits East (17%), Stenn Desert (15%), Okran's Gulf, Okran's Valley, Shem, Sinkuun and Stobe's Garden (13%), Bonefields (10%).

## Border Zone (region)
Also: start area, starting region, no-mans-land
- Border Zone: arid no-man's-land between the Shek Kingdom and The Holy Nation; south of Okran's Pride and east of Vain. Most game starts begin here.
- Neighbours: N Okran's Pride; NW Okran's Gulf; NE Skinner's Roam (est.); E Shem; S The Swamp; SW Spider Plains and Stenn Desert; W Vain.
- Towns: The Hub (centre, ruined outlaw town) and Squin (Shek Kingdom, south-west).
- Outposts: Waystation (Tech Hunters, south-east), Rebel Base (bar just north of The Hub), Dust King Tower (Dust Bandit HQ), Tower Of Ninjas (Black Dragon Ninjas HQ).
- Ruins: Ruined Holy Outpost, Tiny Settlement.
- No faction patrols the region at the start of the game.
- Weather: dust storms about 20% of the time; never damaging.
- Danger for a new squad: low, the lowest in the game.

## Border Zone: who roams there
Also: Border Zone bandits, Border Zone animals, Border Zone threats
- Starving Bandits, the most common squads: mobs of 5-9 Hungry Bandits with 1 Hungry Bandit Leader, or big mobs of 9-16.
- Dust Bandits: squads of 1 Dust Boss, 5-10 Dust Bandits and 0-4 Dust Bandit Bowmen; up to 13 camps and nests exist at once, mostly Dust Bandit Camps.
- Slave Hunters: 1-8 Slavemongers; they pick up and enslave downed strangers.
- Escaped Holy Servants: 1-8 Escaped Servants, starving.
- Animals: lone Bonedogs and Wolf Dens, Wild Goats (1-12), Garru families (2-6).
- Neutral: single Drifters (Tech Hunters), Merchant Nomads caravans, Goat Herder's Camps.
- Rare: a lone Wandering Assassin (Shinobi Thieves).
- Shek, Holy Nation, Kral's Chosen and Hive squads appear only after certain faction leaders are killed.
- Starving Bandits and Dust Bandits also fight each other.

## Border Zone: ore, farming and why to start here
Also: Border Zone mining, Border Zone resources, Border Zone farming
- Prospecting values: iron 100, copper 80, stone 100, water 50; arid fertility 100, green 10.
- Iron veins of 100% quality are almost everywhere, most densely in the southern canyons and on the western edge near Vain.
- The Hub, Squin and Waystation all have iron and copper nodes close by; Waystation has many iron veins plus copper nodes.
- Satellite wreckage near the southern edge can be salvaged for iron.
- Places to trade: Squin, Waystation, The Hub bar, Rebel Base bar.
- Being knocked out here is rarely fatal: Dust King Tower throws out or cages beaten intruders (its two wiki descriptions differ) rather than killing them, and passing slavers may pick up, heal and enslave a dying character.
- A player outpost in the middle of the region draws both Holy Nation and Shek visits.

## Okran's Gulf (region)
Also: Okrans Gulf
- Okran's Gulf: flat, arid but fertile western edge of Holy Nation territory; becomes mountainous toward Rebirth.
- Neighbours: SE Border Zone; E Okran's Pride; NE Rebirth; N Floodlands; W Fog Islands and Dreg; SW Vain (est.).
- Control: The Holy Nation. Town: Stack. Outposts: Holy Military Base x2, Holy Mines x2. Ruin: Deadhive Overrun (Fogmen).
- Roaming: Holy Army Patrols (1 High Paladin, 4-6 Paladins, 6-10 Holy Servants), Inquisitor squads, Starving Bandit mobs (5-9 or 9-16), Escaped Servants, Wild Bull herds (3-6), Bonedogs, Mercenary Guild squads, trade caravans.
- Weather: dust storms about 13% of the time.
- Resources: iron 100, copper 80, arid fertility 100.
- Danger for a new squad: low for human males; high for non-humans, Skeletons and characters with robot limbs because of Holy Nation patrols.

## Okran's Pride (region)
Also: Okrans Pride, green plains
- Okran's Pride: fertile green grassland with plentiful water; the most populated region in the game.
- Neighbours: S Border Zone; W Okran's Gulf; N Rebirth, Wend, Okran's Valley (est.) and Arm of Okran (est.); SE Skinner's Roam (est.).
- Control: The Holy Nation. Towns: Blister Hill (capital, in the north) and Bad Teeth. Outposts: Holy Farm x4.
- Roaming: Holy Army Patrols (very frequent), Inquisitor squads, prisoner caravans, trade caravans, Starving Bandit mobs (5-9), Escaped Servants, lone Bonedogs.
- Wildlife: swarms of River Raptors (River Nests) that eat crops; Wolf Dens.
- Resources: green fertility 100, water 100, iron 100, copper only 20 (few copper nodes).
- Weather: light rain or clear; no hazards.
- Danger for a new squad: low if human and not hostile to The Holy Nation; see Okran's Gulf for who is at risk.

## Skinner's Roam (region)
Also: Skinners Roam, centre of the map
- Skinner's Roam: large rocky desert east and north-east of the Border Zone; a valley in its south is the centre of the world map.
- Neighbours: SW Border Zone; NW Okran's Pride; NE Iron Valleys; E Deadlands; S Shem (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Control: nominally The Holy Nation, which keeps troops only inside its mines. Outposts: Holy Mines x3; Holy Mine Ruin x2 on the Deadlands side.
- Roaming: Starving Bandits, including giant mobs of 40 Hungry Bandits with 2 leaders; Escaped Servants; Dust Bandit Camps.
- Wildlife: Wild Bull herds of 8-16 (or 3-6), lone Bonedogs, Wolf Dens.
- Weather: dust storms about 76% of the time; wear dust-storm headgear.
- Ground and water are not acidic; cactus farms do well.
- Danger for a new squad: moderate (very large bandit mobs, bull herds).

## Stenn Desert (region)
Also: Shek homeland, Stenn
- Stenn Desert: barren rocky desert, home of the Shek Kingdom. It never rains here.
- Neighbours: E/NE Border Zone; W, N and NE Vain; S Spider Plains.
- Town: Admag (Shek capital). Outposts: The Great Fortress (Shek), Waystation (Tech Hunters), New Kralia (Kral's Chosen HQ), Exile Camp (Band of Bones). Ruin: Shek Ruins.
- Roaming: Shek Patrols (1-2 Hundred Guardians, 4-8 Shek Warriors), Shek Scout Squads (4-7), Berserker squads (2-8), Kral's Chosen (5-8), Traders Guild and Hive caravans.
- Wildlife: lone Bonedogs, Wolf Dens, Skin Spiders in groups of 1-3 wandering in from Spider Plains.
- Resources: iron 100, copper 70 (many copper veins are only 10% quality), stone 110, water 20.
- Weather: dust storms about 15% of the time; strong wind.
- Danger for a new squad: moderate (Berserkers and Kral's Chosen attack on sight).

## Vain (region)
Also: hive homeland, red river
- Vain: red-tinted land along the west coast with a red acidic river through the middle; heartland of the Western Hive.
- Neighbours: E Border Zone; NE Okran's Gulf; N Dreg; SE Stenn Desert; S Spider Plains (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Outposts: Hive Village x5 and the Western Hive (queen's hive, far west). Ruins: Lost Library, Post-Ancient Workshop, Tower of Fog.
- Wildlife: Beak Things (nests), Gorillos (most common roaming animal), lone Bonedogs, Garru families, Wild Bulls.
- Also: Escaped Servants (1-8); Hive caravans.
- The river water is acidic to non-Hivers; the weather itself is harmless (red rain has no acid effect).
- Hive village shopkeepers will not trade with "Hiveless" hiver characters; hive village guards do defend visitors against Beak Things and Gorillos.
- Resources: iron and copper normal, mixed arid/green fertility 75.
- Danger for a new squad: high (Beak Things outrun new characters).

## Shem (region)
Also: oasis, base location
- Shem: sandy oasis region east of the Border Zone; isolated and thinly populated.
- Neighbours: W Border Zone; N Skinner's Roam (est.); NE Deadlands; E Venge; S Bonefields and Burning Forest; SW The Swamp.
- Outposts: Settled Nomads (Nomads) and Smugglers Bar (Grayflayers; has many beds). No towns.
- Roaming: small Beak Thing groups (1-3, the most common spawn), Dust Bandit squads and camps, Starving Bandit mobs, Merchant Nomads, small Blood Spider groups, Garru families.
- Resources: iron 100, copper 80, water 70, arid fertility 100.
- Popular place for a player outpost: central, flat, no faction collects taxes there.
- Weather: dust storms about 13% of the time.
- Danger for a new squad: moderate (Beak Things).

## The Swamp (region)
Also: swamp, swamplands
- Foggy, densely overgrown wetland south of the Border Zone; low visibility.
- Neighbours: N Border Zone; NE Shem; E Burning Forest; S South Wetlands; SW The Grid; W Spider Plains.
- Control: Swampers and crime gangs (Hounds, Blackshifters, Grayflayers, Stone Rats, Twinblades).
- Town: Shark. Outposts: Mud Town, Rot, Grayflayer Village, Stone Rat Village, Swamp Village x2, Red Sabre Hideout. Ruins: Infested Lab, Swamped Lab, Lost Library Outpost.
- Roaming: Blood Spiders (1-10), Swamp Turtles (1-3), Red Sabres (7-11), Swamp Ninjas (6-11), Band of Bones; Swamp Raptor Nests.
- Resources: swamp fertility 100 (hemp, riceweed), water 200, 100% copper and iron veins.
- Blood Spiders attack while a squad is fighting something else or wading through shallow water.
- Danger for a new squad: high; the wiki suggests stats of 30+.

## Spider Plains (region)
Also: spider plains
- Spider Plains: large hilly field full of Skin Spiders, south of Stenn Desert.
- Neighbours: N Stenn Desert and Vain (est.); NE Border Zone; E The Swamp; SE The Grid; S Shun (est.); SW Arach and Watcher's Rim (est.).
- Outposts: Last Stand (Shek Kingdom), Berserker Village (Berserkers). Ruin: Old Frontlines.
- Roaming: Skin Spiders (groups of 1-3 or 1-10), Shek Scout Squads and Patrols, Berserker squads (2-8), Kral's Chosen (5-8), Band of Bones (4-8), lone Bonedogs.
- Skin Spiders are slow; an unencumbered character can outrun them. They eat unconscious organic characters.
- Weapons with a bonus against animals or spiders (Desert Sabre, Polearm) help.
- Weather: dust storms about 33% of the time.
- Danger for a new squad: high.

## Dreg (region)
Also: Dreg zone
- Dreg: sparse, "dead" land west of Okran's Gulf and north of Vain; south-west of the Fog Islands.
- Neighbours: E/NE Fog Islands and Okran's Gulf; S Vain; N/NW Raptor Island.
- Outposts: Hive Village x3 (Western Hive), Gatherer Village x2 (Deadcat), Settled Nomad Village (Nomads).
- Roaming: Escaped Servants, lost Fogmen (1-5), Starving Bandit mobs (5-9), Scrawny Cannibals (10-35, occasional), Goat Herders, Merchant Nomads.
- The Hive Exile game start can begin here.
- Resources: low-quality copper, iron 50; nothing grows.
- Danger for a new squad: moderate.

## Fog Islands (region)
Also: fog, foggy islands
- Fog Islands: valleys under a permanent low fog, north-west of the Border Zone beyond Okran's Gulf.
- Neighbours: E Okran's Gulf; SW Dreg; N Obedience and The Shrieking Forest.
- Town: Mongrel (independent, safe inside). Nests: Fog Deathyards.
- Roaming: Fogmen squads of 7-21 (very common), Fog Prince squads (1 Fog Prince, 3-5 Fog Heavies), rare hunting parties of 31-56 Fogmen, Escaped Servants.
- Fogmen carry downed organic characters to a pole and eat them alive, limbs first; downed Skeletons are usually left alone.
- The fog itself does no damage.
- To reach Mongrel, do not fight: run. The wiki says a solo runner with run speed in the low 20s mph makes it, and Mongrel's gate guards kill pursuers.
- Resources: iron 100, copper 100, fertility 80-100.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme outside Mongrel.

## Rebirth (region)
Also: Rebirth mine region
- Mountain region north-west of Blister Hill holding Rebirth, the largest Holy Nation slave mine.
- Neighbours: SW/S Okran's Gulf; E Wend; NW Floodlands.
- Outposts: Rebirth (The Holy Nation) and Flotsam Safehouse (Flotsam Ninjas, north of the mine).
- Non-humans who come here risk being enslaved.
- After escaping the mine, head north to the Flotsam Safehouse: food, hacksaws for shackles and clothes are free to take.
- No resources; no roaming spawns.
- Danger for a new squad: high for non-humans; nothing to gain for others.

## Wend (region)
Also: Wend river
- River valley separating Rebirth (west) from the north-eastern Holy Nation lands.
- Neighbours: S Okran's Pride; W Rebirth; NW Floodlands; N Hidden Forest; NE Arm of Okran (est.).
- Ruins: Drowned Ruins, where the Armour King (a Skeleton armour seller) lives in a half-drowned building; a Lost Library that cannot be reached.
- Roaming: a few Holy Nation Robots (wandering Skeletons).
- A bridge crosses the Wend river north of Blister Hill.
- Water for training Swimming is plentiful.
- Danger for a new squad: low to moderate.

## Okran's Valley (region)
Also: Okrans Valley
- Okran's Valley: valley between the two Arm of Okran mountain zones, north of Okran's Pride; about one third grassland, two thirds desert.
- Neighbours: S Okran's Pride; E Bast; NW Northern Coast; Arm of Okran on both sides (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Control: The Holy Nation. Outposts: Holy Farm x2. Ruin: Farm Ruins.
- Roaming: Holy Army Patrols, Inquisitor squads, Escaped Servants (common), Starving Bandit mobs, lone Bonedogs, occasional Scrawny Cannibals (10-35).
- Weather: dust storms about 13% of the time.
- Danger for a new squad: low to moderate for humans.

## Arm of Okran (region)
Also: Okran's Arm
- Arm of Okran: two green mountain zones west and east of Okran's Valley.
- Western mountain: World's End (Tech Hunters town and Machinists' university).
- Eastern mountain: Holy Nation strongpoints Okran's Fist and Watchtower.
- Neighbours: Okran's Valley between the two; S Okran's Pride; SW Wend; W Hidden Forest; NW Northern Coast; E Bast; SE Skimsands (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- No roaming spawns, nests or camps of its own.
- Resources: green fertility 100, water 150, iron 100, copper 20.
- Danger for a new squad: low inside the zone; the approach through Hidden Forest or Northern Coast has Cannibals.

## Iron Valleys (region)
Also: acid valleys
- Iron Valleys: rocky valleys north of the Deadlands with constant acid rain; some bridges link the high ground.
- Neighbours: SW Skinner's Roam; SE Deadlands and The Black Desert; E Skimsands (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Outposts on its border with Skinner's Roam: Okran's Shield and Narko's Trap (The Holy Nation). Ruin: Lost Armoury.
- Roaming: single Iron Spiders (most common), single Broken Skeletons, Skeleton Drifters, Black Desert Ninjas (3 Cloud Ninjas).
- Water is acidic; ground is 50% acidic, so barefoot non-immune characters are hurt, less than in the Deadlands.
- No resources.
- Danger for a new squad: high without acid protection and boots.

## Deadlands (region)
Also: acid rain desert, dead lands
- Deadlands: one of the most polluted regions: constant acid rain, acidic ground, acid lakes, darkness.
- Neighbours: W Skinner's Roam (est.); NW Iron Valleys (est.); E Grey Desert; SE Venge; SW Shem.
- Town: Black Desert City (Skeletons). Outpost: Scraphouse, next to it. Ruins: Deadland Workshop x3, Lost Armoury x3, Armoury Ruin.
- Roaming: only single or paired Iron Spiders. No bandits.
- Humans and Shek need rain protection (Iron Hat or Ashland Hat plus Longcoat or Dustcoat), boots for the ground, and must not swim in the lakes.
- Hivers, Skeletons and animals are immune to the acid.
- Build a tent to rest outdoors.
- No wind, no resources, and rain collectors catch nothing.
- Danger for a new squad: high for unprotected humans and Shek; moderate for Hivers and Skeletons who avoid Iron Spiders.

## The Black Desert (region)
Also: poison gas desert, black clouds
- Dark sand next to the Deadlands and Iron Valleys with drifting black poison gas clouds.
- Neighbours: SW Deadlands; NW Iron Valleys; N Skimsands; E The Eye; SE Grey Desert (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- No towns, outposts or ruins.
- Roaming: Black Desert Ninjas (3-9 Cloud Ninjas).
- Gas does stun damage to the chest (rarely the stomach); an unprotected Greenlander goes from 100 to 0 in about 14 game minutes inside a cloud.
- The ground is acidic: wear shoes. Water is acidic.
- Characters cough and say "my lungs..." when inside a cloud: walk out of it.
- Resources: iron and copper at double richness.
- Danger for a new squad: high.

## Grey Desert (region)
Also: gray desert
- Grey Desert: small, mostly empty desert bordering the polluted centre.
- Neighbours: W Deadlands; NW The Black Desert (est.); NE The Eye (est.); S Venge.
- Outpost: Waystation (Tech Hunters), the only resupply point when approaching Black Desert City from the east.
- Roaming: Traders Guild caravans, Grayflayer smuggler caravans, Merchant Nomads, single Skimmers, Bloodraiders (3-11), Rebel Farmers, Black Desert Ninjas (3-9), Slaver caravans, single Iron Spiders, Black Dog extortionists (4-6), Weak Thralls from Venge (2-22).
- No acid or gas here.
- Danger for a new squad: moderate.

## Venge (region)
Also: laser region, burning beams region
- Heated valleys and mountains south of the Grey Desert, swept by laser beams during the day.
- Neighbours: N Grey Desert and The Eye (est.); W Shem; NE Stormgap Coast; E The Unwanted Zone (est.); SE Stobe's Gamble; SW Flats Lagoon.
- Outposts: Tower of Abuse (Thrall Masters), Old Control Tower (Scavengers). Ruins: Ruined Control Tower, Waystation Ruins.
- Roaming: Weak Thralls in squads of 2-22 (main danger), small Beak Thing groups, Blood Spiders (1-3), Iron Spiders.
- Beams do heavy cutting ("burning") damage to organic characters; Skeletons are immune.
- Cross at night: the wiki advises entering at 23:00 and being under cover by 05:00.
- Being inside any building, including the top floor, blocks beam damage.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme by day, high by night.

## Skimsands (region)
Also: skimmer desert
- Skimsands: buffer desert between the United Cities and The Holy Nation, infested with Skimmers.
- Neighbours: N Bast and Spine Canyon; NE The Great Desert; E Heng; SE The Eye; S The Black Desert; W Iron Valleys; NW Arm of Okran (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Outpost: Tengu's Vault (United Cities prison).
- Roaming: Skimmer packs of 3-6 at night and lone Skimmers at any hour (by far the most common), Rebel Farmers, Manhunters (5-14), Holy Army forces, United Cities army patrols.
- Skimmer Nests are common.
- Almost no natural resources.
- Weather: dust storms about 68% of the time.
- Danger for a new squad: high.

## Spine Canyon (region)
Also: spine canyons
- Spine Canyon: long rocky plateau separating Skimsands from The Great Desert.
- Neighbours: NE The Great Desert; S and W Skimsands (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Outpost: Cactus Den (Sand Ninjas HQ). Camps: Blood Bandit Camps.
- Roaming: Bloodraiders (3-11), Sand Ninja cells (3-5), single Sand Ninja Jounin, Bonedog packs at night (1-6).
- Near-constant sandstorms: good for wind power, stat penalties for unprotected characters (dust storms about 68% of the time).
- Danger for a new squad: high.

## The Great Desert (region)
Also: great desert, big desert
- The Great Desert: vast arid desert in the north-east corner of the continent; United Cities core.
- Neighbours: NW Sinkuun; W Bast; SW Spine Canyon (est.) and Skimsands (est.); S Heng.
- Towns: Heft (capital), Stoat, Sho-Battai, Bark. Outposts: Fort Mirage, Port North, Port South, Stone Camp. Ruin: Old Village.
- Roaming: Skimmers (packs of 3-6 at night, loners any time), Rebel Farmers (up to 16), Starving Vagrants (1-13), Manhunters (5-14), Sand Ninjas (3-5), slave and trade caravans, samurai patrols.
- Camps: Raider Camps, Vagrant Camps; Skimmer Nests.
- The Rock Bottom game start begins here, east of Sho-Battai.
- Resources: iron 100, copper 70, stone 110, water 20.
- Weather: dust storms about 78% of the time.

## Greenbeach (region)
Also: green beach
- Greenbeach: east-coast region controlled by the Crab Raiders; full of Crabs.
- Neighbours: NW The Outlands; W Stobe's Garden; S The Pits East (unverified: directions are this file's estimate)
- Outpost: Crab Village. Nests: Crab Nest.
- Roaming: Crabs (solo, groups, occasional massive Crab), Crab Raider patrols, Reaver patrols, small Beak Thing groups.
- Resources: green fertility 100, iron 100, copper 55.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme.

## Sonorous Dark (region)
Also: Sonorous
- Sonorous Dark: start of the Ashlands peninsula in the far south-east; home of the Skin Bandits.
- Neighbours: NW Sniper Valley (est.); SW Cheaters Run; SE Ashlands; S Sonorous Junk Reef.
- Outposts: Skinhouse HQ, Skinhouse x2. Ruin: Barrier Tower (Skeleton Legion).
- Roaming: Skin Bandit patrols (8-20; most common), Skeleton Legion scouts (2-9), Iron Spider groups (4-18), Sniperbots, Black Gorillos.
- Skin Bandits kidnap unconscious organic characters and peel them; pick up downed squadmates at once.
- Weather: acid rain about 50% of the time; water is acidic.
- Resources: iron 200, copper 150, stone 200.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme.

## Sonorous Junk Reef (region)
Also: junk reef
- Sonorous Junk Reef: shallow water south of Sonorous Dark and west of the Ashlands, filled with scrap and inactive Iron Spiders.
- No spawns and no hostiles; it lies outside the normal regions.

## Ashlands (region)
Also: ash lands, end game region
- Ashlands: ash-covered peninsula in the far south-east corner; domain of the Second Empire.
- Neighbours: N The Pits; NW Sonorous Dark; W Sonorous Junk Reef.
- Locations: Cat-Lon's Exile, Ashland Dome I-IV, Ashland Dome Ruins.
- Roaming: Skeleton Legion forces (7-22 Legions with 2-7 Legion Scouts), Legion Thrall units (1 Legion, 18-35 Legion Thralls), Screamer Units (6-15), Iron Spider groups (4-18), single Cleanser Units.
- Large white poison gas clouds: bring a Fog Mask or other 100% gas protection. Gas does not form inside Cat-Lon's Exile.
- Worth going for: Research Artifacts and Meitou and Edgewalker grade weapons.
- Routes: Stobe's Gamble, then Sniper Valley, then around the Skinhouse; or Cheaters Run and the Sonorous Dark coast.
- Resources: iron 300, copper 300.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme (end-game region).

## Forbidden Isle (region)
Also: forbidden island
- Forbidden Isle: island east of Stormgap Coast (its only neighbour), swarming with Iron Spiders from the Spider Factory at its centre.
- Ruins: Spider Factory, Ancient Labs.
- Roaming: Iron Spider groups of 4-12 while the Spider Foreman lives; aggressive Tech Hunter ruin squads (1-5).
- Weather: acid rain about 50% of the time.
- Danger for a new squad: extreme.

## Islands of None (region)
Also: unnamed islands east
- Islands of None: unnamed islands east of Howler Maze and north of Forbidden Isle, reachable only by swimming.
- Roaming: single Crabs.
- Weather: acid rain about 18% of the time.
- Little usable land; inconvenient to reach.

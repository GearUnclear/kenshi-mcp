# Towns

Each town, city, outpost and notable location in Kenshi: its region and controlling faction, who is unwelcome, which shops and services it has, nearby resources, and how to reach it from neighbouring towns.

## How town directions and distances were worked out
Also: map scale, how far, compass, straight line distance, accuracy of distances
- Directions (N, NNE, NE...) and distances were computed from marker coordinates on the Fandom interactive Location map. They are straight lines; real paths bend around cliffs and rivers. North is up on the in-game map.
- Scale: the world is a 64 x 64 grid of map zones, each 4,608 game units across (meitouclient notes), and 1 unit is 0.1 m (wiki), so the map is about 29.5 km wide. The Hub to Squin is about 1.8 km.
- Shop lists are the wiki's lists for the untouched town; killing faction leaders can change a town.

## Travel time between towns
Also: how long to walk, game hours, real minutes, run speed, journey time
- A new character (Athletics 0, unencumbered) runs at 15.7 mph, or 7 m/s: 1 km takes about 2.4 minutes of real time at normal game speed.
- One game hour lasts about 109 real seconds at normal speed, so a game day is about 43.6 minutes (meitouclient notes). 1 km is therefore about 1.3 game hours for a new runner; The Hub to Squin is 4-5 real minutes or about 2.4 game hours.
- Encumbrance, carrying a body and detours around cliffs all add time.
- Faster game speeds shorten the real time, not the game hours.

## Shop types: general goods, travel gear and building supplies
Also: general shop, travel gear shop, mechanical shop, where to buy building materials
- General Shop (Squin, Admag, Mongrel, Catun, Clownsteady, Drifter's Last, Bark, Last Stand): Building Materials, Iron Plates, Basic First Aid Kit, Fabrics, Leather, Steel Bars, Water, Hacksaw, Skeleton Repair Kit and other materials; up to 180 items, restocks every 24 hours, open by day.
- Travel Gear (Squin, Admag, Heft, Sho-Battai, Last Stand, Farming Village): first aid and splint kits, Ration Pack, Sleeping Bag, backpacks, crossbow bolts, tent blueprints, and maps including Map of the Border Zone; 100 items, restocks every 24 hours.
- Adventurer Mechanical Shop (Waystations, World's End, Flats Lagoon, Black Scratch): construction materials and all crops except Riceweed; 150 items.
- Holy Nation and United Cities towns list a Mechanical Shop of their own; its stock was not checked.

## Shop types: food, crossbows, robotics and armour
Also: farm shop, ranger shop, robotics workshop, armour shop, where to buy a crossbow
- Farm Shop (Heft, Heng, Stoat, Bark, Brink, Black Scratch, Farming Village, Fishing Village): crops, Bread, Dried Meat, Raw Meat, animal backpacks, and a Garru or Bonedog for sale.
- Ranger Shop: crossbows and bolts. Mongrel, Flats Lagoon, World's End, Black Scratch, Spring, Clownsteady, Heft, Sho-Battai, Bark, Catun. None in Shek Kingdom or Holy Nation towns.
- Robotics Workshop (Waystation in the Border Zone, Heft, Heng, Black Scratch; similar shops in Mongrel and Black Desert City): Robot Limbs and a Skeleton repair bed; only 25 items.
- Tech Hunter Shop (Black Scratch, Mourn, Spring): travel supplies plus light and medium armour.
- Hive Trader: see "Hive Villages (Western Hive)".

## Bars: food, recruits, mercenaries and beds
Also: bar, barman, tavern, what bars offer
- Almost every town has a bar; the Barman sells alcohol, bread and other food (the Skeleton bar in Black Desert City sells no food).
- Bars are where recruits, unique recruits and hireable mercenaries (or Tech Hunters, Cannibal Hunters) wait.
- A Thief Fence in a bar buys stolen goods.
- Beds can be rented in friendly towns and outposts from as little as c.50; a rental lasts 24 hours and squad members can take turns in the bed during that time.
- A bed heals at 8x the normal rate; a Camp Bed (an unfolded Sleeping Bag placed with the build menu outside towns) heals at 4x.
- Sleeping cuts hunger rate to one third.
- Shek Bar stock (Squin, Admag): Cactus Rum, Dried Fish, Dried Meat, Meatwrap, Water, Water Jug.

## Police Stations: which towns have one
Also: police station locations, police chief, jail, where is the police, bounty towns
- Police Stations are tower buildings with a sign by the door.
- United Cities: Brink, Catun, Clownsteady, Heft, Heng, Sho-Battai, Stoat.
- Shek Kingdom: Admag, Squin, Last Stand, The Great Fortress.
- The Holy Nation: Blister Hill, Stack.
- The Hub, Rebel Base, the Waystations, Mongrel and the swamp towns have none.
- They pay bounties: how to hand a wanted character in is under "Turning in a bounty" in Making money.
- If your own character has a bounty, stay away from towns with police.

## Thieves Guild (Shinobi Thieves tower)
Also: thieves tower, ninja tower, Shinobi Trader, Shinobi Thieves Watchtower
- Thieves Guilds are watchtowers of the Shinobi Thieves in The Hub, Admag, Mongrel, Heng, Heft, Sho-Battai, Catun and Clownsteady.
- The Thief Boss stands at the tower; joining and what members get are under "Shinobi Thieves" in Factions.
- The Shinobi Trader inside is a fence: buys and sells anything at half price regardless of where it came from.
- Stock (15 items, restocks every 12 hours): dark light armour (Ninja Rags, Armoured Rags, chain shirts), Ninja Blade, Wakizashi, Katana, Thieves Backpack.
- The tower is open day and night.

## Holy Nation towns: who is unwelcome
Also: Holy Nation town rules, visiting Holy Nation towns, who Holy Nation towns turn away
- The Holy Nation is hostile toward non-humans (Shek, Hivers, Skeletons), the Shek Kingdom, United Cities, Flotsam Ninjas, Mongrel and Holy Nation Outlaws.
- Skeletons are treated as agents of darkness; anyone with a robot limb is taken for a disguised Skeleton.
- Non-humans are normally sent to Rebirth; they are tolerated as servants under a trusted human escort.
- Women may trade and travel, but need a male escort when travelling at night, handing in criminals or setting up outposts.
- Citizens must carry a copy of The Holy Flame; carrying one makes guard dialogue go better.
- Hashish is contraband (the wiki lists it at 400% price there); alcohol is frowned upon.
- If a priest questions a female character, do not insult him (arrest, then Rebirth). Do not bribe guards or mention Narko.
- Safest visitor: a human male carrying The Holy Flame.

## United Cities towns: who is unwelcome
Also: United Cities town rules, visiting United Cities towns, Empire town rules
- Money is the measure of a person: the very poor are treated as probable criminals and are often enslaved.
- Hashish, Hemp and Bloodrum are illegal to carry or bring in.
- A player outpost with hemp buildings in United Cities land triggers a Drug Bust raid.
- Hostile toward Anti-Slavers and The Holy Nation.
- Lawbreakers are caged and can be handed to slavers; in Drifter's Last they are sent to Slave Farm South.
- City Heroes (United Heroes League) assault non-humans in the streets; the guards treat City Heroes as bandits, so fighting back is legal.
- Entering a Noble House counts as trespassing.
- Outside the walls, Manhunters and Slave Hunters enslave anyone they find unconscious.

## Shek Kingdom towns: who is unwelcome
Also: Shek town rules, visiting Shek towns, flatskin, Shek gate search
- Hashish is outlawed; gate guards search characters for it.
- Shek look down on Humans and Hivers and may harass them in town; Skeletons are the one non-Shek race they do not despise.
- The kingdom is hostile toward The Holy Nation. Roaming Shek squads start fights unprovoked with a player who is allied to The Holy Nation.
- Alliance comes at 50 relations and is impossible while allied to The Holy Nation, United Cities, Traders Guild or Slave Traders; Bayan in Admag explains how.
- Shek towns have no Ranger Shop (no crossbows).
- Shek bars are staffed only by Shek.

## The Hub (town)
Also: Hub, starting town, ruined town
- The Hub is a mostly ruined former Holy Nation town near the centre of the Border Zone. Faction: Holy Nation Outlaws.
- Start of the Wanderer, The Holy Sword and Son of a Captain game starts (and sometimes Nobodies).
- Buildings: Bar (a Black Market Bar run by the Trade Ninjas), Thieves Guild (Shinobi Thieves watchtower at the far end of town), one intact Small Shack.
- No Police Station, no general store, no armour or weapon shop.
- Hobbs, a recruit, may be in the bar.
- The Hounds pacifier (diplomat) can be found here.
- Map of the Border Zone reveals the town.
- Ore nodes nearby: "Where to mine near The Hub" in Making money.

## The Hub: gates, guards and safety
Also: Hub guards, is the Hub safe, Hub gates, bandits in the Hub
- The Hub's two town gates are ruins that cannot close (meitouclient's reading of the level data), so there is no gate to shelter behind.
- Roaming Starving Bandit and Dust Bandit squads are set to approach towns: their patrols head for a point about 20 m outside a town gate (meitouclient notes), so expect them at The Hub's gates.
- Fighters present: 2-4 Ninja Guards in the bar; a Thief Boss, 3-5 Thieves and 2-3 Ninja Guards at the Thieves Guild tower.
- Only about 10-12 characters belong to the town's own faction, against 36 in Squin and about 93-95 in Stack (meitouclient notes, read from saves).
- Thieves and Ninja Guards defend Shinobi Thieves members (joining costs c.10,000): pursuers led to the tower are killed. Dust Bandit Camps can spawn near town.
- A Hungry Bandit squats in town under the Holy Nation Outlaws faction.

## The Hub: beds
Also: sleep in the Hub, heal in the Hub, free bed
- The intact Small Shack has 2 beds that speed healing; some NPCs may attack if they see you enter it.
- Shinobi Thieves members may use the beds in the Thieves Guild tower.
- A Camp Bed (an unfolded Sleeping Bag) heals at half the rate of a real bed and can be placed outside town.

## The Hub: bar, selling and buying
Also: Hub bar, Black Market Bar, sell ore Hub, sell loot Hub, buy food Hub
- The bar is a Black Market Bar: up to 70 items, restocks every 24 hours, open day and night.
- It buys anything, including stolen goods, except goods stolen from the Trade Ninjas themselves.
- Food stock includes Bread, Cooked Vegetables and Dried Fish; also Basic First Aid Kit, Building Materials, crossbow bolts.
- Weapons appear rarely (Iron Club, Plank, Katana, Staff and others); crossbows: Tooth Pick (common), Junkbow (uncommon).
- The Shinobi Trader in the Thieves Guild buys and sells at half price.
- For proper shops go to Squin (SW, about 1.8 km) or the Waystation (ESE, about 2.1 km).
- The same bar type stands at Rebel Base, about 0.4 km north.

## The Hub: houses for sale
Also: buy a house in the Hub, Hub house prices, Hub shack
- All houses for sale are collapsed: Small Shack x6 (c.1,500 each), Storm House x2 (c.4,800), Old Storm House x3 (c.6,000), Longhouse x3 (c.7,200), L-House x2 and Bughouse (c.10,500), Snailhouse and Stationhouse (c.12,000).
- Collapsed houses need Building Materials to repair before they hold much.
- A Research Bench can be built inside an owned house, so research can start before leaving town.
- Buying the house nearest the Shinobi Thieves tower means the thieves run out to defend you, if you are allied with them.
- A valley to the west suits an outpost just outside the walls.

## Squin (town)
Also: Shek town, Squin shops, sell ore Squin, police Squin
- Squin is a Shek Kingdom town in a narrow canyon in the south-west of the Border Zone.
- Directly east of Admag (about 2.7 km); SW of The Hub (about 1.8 km).
- Shops: Armour Shop, Bar x2 (Shek Bar), General Shop, Travel Gear, Weapon Shop (Shek Weapon Shop), Police Station.
- Bounties can be handed in at the Police Station (Hundred Guardian).
- About 36 residents belong to the Shek Kingdom (meitouclient notes, from a save).
- Iron and copper veins can be mined nearby.
- Dust Bandits and Hungry Bandits roam outside.
- Houses for sale: Longhouse x2 collapsed (c.7,200 each), Longhouse x2 intact (c.19,200 each).
- Unique recruits who may be in the bar: Horse, Oron, Rane the Giant, Ruka, Kang.
- The Flotsam Ninjas pacifier is here. Map of the Shek Kingdom reveals the town.
- Other routes: Waystation (Stenn Desert) SW 1.2 km; Rot ESE 2.6 km; Shark SSE 3.6 km.

## Stack (town)
Also: Stack shops, Stack city
- Stack is a town. Region: Okran's Gulf, on the edge of Holy Nation territory. Faction: The Holy Nation.
- NNW of The Hub (about 1.7 km); Bad Teeth is ENE 2.4 km; Squin SSW 3.1 km.
- Shops: Armour Shop, Bakery, Bar x3, Boot Shop, Clothes Shop, General Shop, Headgear Shop, Mechanical Shop.
- Other buildings: Armory, Barracks x2, General Storage, Police Station (with the throne of High Inquisitor Seta), Power Station, well.
- No Weapon Shop is listed.
- Houses for sale: Small Shack x2 collapsed (c.1,500), Small Shack x2 (c.4,000).
- Surroundings: Starving Bandits, Bonedogs and Dust Bandits, all weak enemies; a decent training area.
- Holy Nation rules apply: see "Holy Nation towns: who is unwelcome".
- One of two Holy Nation Citizen start towns; selling starting armour at the Boot Shop and buying Wooden Sandals is the wiki's opening tip.
- Glorious Holy Map reveals the town.

## Rebel Base (outpost)
Also: Rebel Base bar, Black Market Bar outpost
- Rebel Base is a one-building outpost in the Border Zone, about 0.4 km N of The Hub. Listed under Holy Nation Outlaws; the only occupants are Trade Ninjas.
- A single building: a Black Market Bar, same stock type as The Hub's bar (crossbows, weapon blueprints, Building Materials, food including Greenfruit).
- Buys stolen goods from any faction but its own.
- Two copper nodes sit next to it: a good first mining spot with a buyer beside it.
- Its Ninja Guards often fight bandits that pass.
- Caution when leading enemies here: the guards side with law enforcement (for example Holy Nation paladins) against the player.

## Waystation (Border Zone)
Also: Border Zone Waystation, Waystation shops, nearest Waystation
- Region: Border Zone, south-east/east part. Faction: Tech Hunters; Mercenary Guild members also live here.
- ESE of The Hub (about 2.1 km); Settled Nomads SE 1.9 km; Rot SSW 2.2 km; Bad Teeth N 3.1 km.
- Buildings: Adventurer Bar, Adventurer Mechanical Shop, Robotics Workshop (the only Waystation with one).
- Adventurer Bar stock: first aid and splint kits, Skeleton Repair Kit, Ration Pack, Foodcube, Meatwrap, Bread, Dried Meat, Sleeping Bag, backpacks, bolts, Tools, maps; 100 items, restocks every 24 hours, open day and night.
- Many iron veins and some copper nodes nearby.
- Good place to rest after a bandit attack and to sell loot from Tiny Settlement and Ruined Holy Outpost.
- 25% chance to hold pacifiers of the United Cities, Traders Guild, The Holy Nation and Shek Kingdom.
- Map of the Border Zone reveals it.

## Hive Villages (Western Hive)
Also: hive village, Hive Trader, hive shop
- Five villages in Vain and three in Dreg. One stands about 1.4 km WNW of The Hub; several lie 1-3 km west, north-west and north of Admag; two are 2-2.5 km west of Mongrel.
- Buildings: Hive Trader, Hive Robotics Shop, factory, hive huts.
- Hive Trader stock: hive shirts, Animal Skin, Building Materials, Iron Plates, Fabrics, Leather, Dried Fish, Raw Meat, Hashish, Hemp, Standard First Aid Kit; 150 items, restocks every 6 hours, open day and night.
- Hive Traders refuse the whole squad if it contains a hiver character ("hiveless"); trade with a squad of humans, Shek or Skeletons only.
- Beak Things and Gorillos often attack the villages in Vain. Villagers fight an animal only once it attacks one of them.
- Hashish bought here is contraband in Holy Nation, United Cities and Shek towns.

## Dust King Tower (hostile outpost)
Also: Dust Bandit HQ, Dust Bandits headquarters
- Dust King Tower is a hostile outpost. Region: Border Zone, on a cliff edge above the river; about 1.5 km ENE of The Hub and 1.3 km NNW of the Waystation.
- Faction: Dust Bandits. Defences: level 3 walls, a gate, 5 mounted crossbows.
- Garrison by the wiki's resident lists: the Dust King, 5-6 Dust Bosses, 9-16 Dust Bandits and 1-5 bowmen; its text says up to 20 or more bandits.
- Cat, a unique recruit, is held in a cage on the third floor.
- A weak squad that enters is swarmed. The tower's page says the downed are locked in cages on the third floor; the Border Zone page says they are thrown out. Neither says they are killed.
- The Dust King's bounty can be claimed early by hiring one or two Mercenary Guild squads and opening the gate for them.
- Unlock the gate from the side by the staircase, out of sight of the crossbows.

## Tower Of Ninjas (hostile outpost)
Also: Black Dragon Ninjas HQ, ninja tower
- Tower Of Ninjas is a hostile outpost. Region: north-east Border Zone; about 2.6 km ENE of The Hub, 1.5 km SSE of Bad Teeth.
- Home of the Black Dragon Ninjas; laid out like a Shinobi Thieves tower.
- Entering the tower turns the faction hostile.
- The ninjas also attack Dust Bandits and similar mobs that are led to them.
- A Tech Hunters Map reveals it.

## Ruined Holy Outpost and Tiny Settlement (ruins)
Also: free loot ruins, lootable ruins near the start
- Ruined Holy Outpost: small ruin between The Hub and Tiny Settlement, about 1.2 km ESE of The Hub; weapons lie on the ground, plus a few Iron Plates.
- Tiny Settlement: deserted Holy Nation outpost on a mountain at the eastern edge of the Border Zone, about 2.7 km E of The Hub and 0.8 km ENE of the Waystation.
- Tiny Settlement has three ruined buildings, no residents and no guards.
- Typical Tiny Settlement loot: about 18 Building Materials, 19 Iron Plates, 5 Fuel, 4 Tools, 3 Gears, books, and low-quality swords.
- Sell the haul at the Waystation.
- The Holy Mine Ruins in Skinner's Roam hold a similar selection.

## Holy Mines (outposts)
Also: Holy Nation slave mines, Holy Mine
- Five slave-labour mines of The Holy Nation: two in Okran's Gulf, three in Skinner's Roam.
- One lies about 1.1 km SW of Bad Teeth (between The Hub and Bad Teeth); one about 1.8 km NE of the Border Zone Waystation; one about 2.2 km E of Mongrel.
- The overseer may enslave anyone lying unconscious near the gates.
- Captured enemies from the surrounding area are put to work there.
- Glorious Holy Map reveals them.

## Admag (town)
Also: Shek capital, Admag shops
- Admag is a town. Region: Stenn Desert, on a hilltop with a single entrance. Faction: Shek Kingdom (capital).
- Directly west of Squin (about 2.7 km); about 4.0 km WSW of The Hub.
- Shops: Armour Shop, Bar x2, General Shop, Travel Gear, Weapon Shop, Thieves Guild.
- Police Station x3 for bounties.
- Faction HQ: Esata the Stone Golem with Bayan and Seto, guarded by The Five Invincibles.
- Defence: Hundred Guardians in town; Shek Warriors patrol the desert.
- For sale: Watchtower collapsed (c.21,000), Watchtower (c.56,000).
- Unique recruits who may be in the bar: Oron, Ruka, Rane the Giant, Kang.
- Nearby: The Great Fortress SSE 1.1 km; Waystation (Stenn Desert) ESE 2.1 km; Last Stand SSW 3.0 km.
- Map of the Shek Kingdom reveals it.

## Waystation (Stenn Desert)
Also: Stenn Desert Waystation, Shek waystation
- Region: easternmost Stenn Desert. Faction: Tech Hunters; residents include Mercenary Guild members and Drifters.
- About 1.2 km SW of Squin; Admag is WNW 2.1 km.
- Buildings: Bar and Mechanical Shop only.
- A place to hide after picking up a small bounty with the Shek Kingdom.
- The Exile Camp of the Band of Bones is only about 0.4 km south: its exiles chase off anyone who approaches.
- 25% chance of faction pacifiers, like every Waystation.

## The Great Fortress and Last Stand (Shek outposts)
Also: Shek fortress, Shek forts
- The Great Fortress: Stenn Desert, about 1.1 km SSE of Admag; partly ruined; has a Police Station.
- Last Stand: Spider Plains, about 3.0 km SSW of Admag; a fortress in two walled areas.
- Last Stand first area: Bar, Police Station, two Faction HQ buildings.
- Last Stand second area: four Police Stations, Shek Weapon Shop, Shek Armoury; also General Shop and Travel Gear per the shop pages.
- Both are revealed by Map of the Shek Kingdom.
- Skin Spiders roam around Last Stand.

## Hostile camps in Shek lands (New Kralia, Exile Camp, Berserker Village)
Also: Kral's Chosen camp, Band of Bones camp, Berserker camp, rogue Shek bases

## Bad Teeth (town)
Also: Bad Teeth shops, mountain pass town
- Bad Teeth is a town. Region: Okran's Pride, guarding the pass toward Skinner's Roam. Faction: The Holy Nation.
- NE of The Hub (about 2.9 km); Blister Hill N 2.4 km; Stack WSW 2.4 km; Waystation (Border Zone) S 3.1 km.
- Shops: Armour Shop, Weapon Shop, Bakery, Bar x3.
- Other buildings: Armory, Barracks x2, General Storage, Temple, Power Station, well. No Police Station is listed.

## Blister Hill (town)
Also: Holy Nation capital, Blister Hill shops
- Blister Hill is a town. Region: north of Okran's Pride. Faction: The Holy Nation (capital); seat of Holy Lord Phoenix.
- Bad Teeth S 2.4 km; Stack SSW 3.6 km; World's End NNE 4.5 km; Rebirth NW 1.6 km.
- Split by an inner wall: throne room and military buildings inside, general town outside.
- Shops: Armour Shop, Weapon Shop, Bakery, Bar x3, Boot Shop, Clothes Shop, Headgear Shop, Mechanical Shop.

## Holy Farms and Holy Military Bases
Also: Holy Farm, Holy Nation farms
- Six Holy Farms: four in Okran's Pride (two within about 1.1 km of Bad Teeth, two within 1.7 km of Blister Hill), two in Okran's Valley.
- Farms are staffed by Greenlander farmers with some soldiers.
- Two Holy Military Bases stand on the western edge of Okran's Gulf facing the Fog Islands: about 1.7 km NNW of Stack and 1.5 km WSW of Blister Hill.
- Glorious Holy Map reveals all of them.

## Rebirth and Flotsam Safehouse
Also: Rebirth slave mine, escape Rebirth, safehouse
- Rebirth: Holy Nation labour camp in a mountain pit, about 1.6 km NW of Blister Hill; all races except Skeletons are held there.
- Start of The Slaves game start.
- Flotsam Safehouse: a single shack north of Rebirth, about 2.2 km NNW of Blister Hill and 2.2 km SSW of Flotsam Village.
- The safehouse holds low-level gear, a decent amount of food, hacksaws and 2 beds; taking them has no penalty.
- Escape route: Rebirth, then north to the safehouse, then on to Flotsam Village.

## Mongrel (town)
Also: Fog Islands town, outlaw town, Mongrel shops
- Region: middle of the Fog Islands. Faction: Mongrel (independent).
- NW of The Hub (about 5.2 km); Stack is SE 3.6 km; Blister Hill E 4.6 km.
- Shops: Armour Shop, Weapon Shop, General Shop, Bar x3, Boot Shop, Clothes Shop, Ranger Shop, Robotics Workshop, Tech Workshop, Shinobi Thieves Watchtower.
- Sells goods not available in Holy Nation towns.
- For sale: Small Shack x2 (c.4,000), Y-House (c.14,000).
- Powerful gate guards fight constant Fogmen attacks; a Fogman sometimes slips in and carries off a sleeping squad member, who cannot resist while carried.
- Unique recruits: Beep, Crumblejon, Infinite Wingwang, Shryke.
- Buy a Map to Mongrel first; run the whole way.
- Entering private houses here counts as trespassing.

## Shark (town)
Also: swamp town, swamp capital, Shark shops
- Region: centre of The Swamp. Faction: Swampers; the Hounds gang (44-47 Hounds in their HQ) really runs it.
- Squin is NNW about 3.6 km; Mud Town NNE 0.9 km; Rot NNE 1.9 km; Clownsteady SSW 4.6 km.
- Shops: The Dancing Skeleton (bar), Blackshifter Casino Bar (gambling), general Shop, Shark Travel Shop, Shark Smithy, Shark Mechanical Shop, Fish and Drugs Seller.
- Hemp and riceweed farms in town.
- For sale: Swamp Shack x3 (c.4,000), Swamp Store (c.6,400), Swamp House x2 (c.16,000).
- No Police Station.
- Dangers in town: Blood Spider attacks, drunk Bar Thugs, Red Sabre and Swamp Ninja assaults.
- The Holy Nation pacifier is here.
- Map of the Swamps reveals it.

## Mud Town and Rot (swamp villages)
Also: swamp villages, Mud Town bar
- Mud Town: about 0.9 km NNE of Shark; Swampers village; bar run by the Blackshifters; a shop; a hashish dealer (Fish and Drugs Seller); Twinblades hideout (leader Big Fang).
- Mud Town can spawn the most unique recruits of any swamp settlement: Doctor Chung, Green, Stubs Momuso.
- Mud Town for sale: residential buildings x2 (c.4,000), Swamp Dome (c.32,000).
- Rot: about 1.9 km NNE of Shark and 2.2 km SSW of the Border Zone Waystation; run by the Hounds; Big Shop and a Fish and Drugs Seller; Swamp House for sale (c.16,000).
- Rot is the first swamp village reached coming south from the Border Zone.
- Both are revealed by Map of the Swamps.

## Other swamp villages (Swamp Village, Stone Rat Village, Grayflayer Village)
Also: swamp rest stop, hashish dealer
- Two Swamp Villages (Swampers): each has a Big Shop and a hashish dealer; safe rest stops with guards. One is about 0.7 km SW of Shark; the other lies on the swamp's northern edge, about 1.7 km S of Squin.
- Stone Rat Village: about 1.4 km SSE of Shark; Traveler's Shop and hashish dealer; leader Big Al.
- Grayflayer Village: about 0.9 km E of Mud Town; Swamp Store (Fish and Drugs Seller); leader Big Gray.

## Settled Nomads and Smugglers Bar (Shem)
Also: Nomad Animal Shop, Settled Nomad Village, animal shop
- Settled Nomads: small Nomads village in Shem, about 1.9 km SE of the Border Zone Waystation and 3.9 km ESE of The Hub.
- Its Nomad Animal Shop sells every kind of domesticated animal (Bonedogs, Pack Bulls, Garru pack beasts, Goats).
- A second Nomad village with the same shop is in Dreg (Settled Nomad Village, about 3.2 km WNW of Mongrel); it also sells Garru Backpacks and Bull Backpacks.
- Smugglers Bar: Grayflayers bar in Shem, about 1.9 km ENE of Settled Nomads and 2.9 km SSW of Black Desert City.
- Smugglers Bar has many free beds upstairs, a Smuggler Barman and 6 Grayflayers; recruits and mercenaries spawn here.
- Smugglers Bar is the nearest shelter west of Venge.

## Black Desert City and Scraphouse
Also: Skeleton town, BDC, Scraphouse shop
- Black Desert City: middle of the Deadlands; faction Skeletons. Waystation (Grey Desert) is E 2.1 km; Smugglers Bar SSW 2.9 km; Bad Teeth W 4.2 km.
- Buildings: Bar (no food for sale; regular beds and Skeleton repair beds upstairs), Armory, Robotics Shop, storage houses.
- For sale: Metal Wallhouse (c.16,000).
- No Police Station: a character knocked out in a fight is carried outside, into the acid rain.
- Constant acid rain and acidic ground on the approach: see wildlife-and-hazards.md.
- Scraphouse: weapons and goods shop in an old factory about 0.3 km E of the city, across a bridge; shopkeeper Quin; guarded by 8 Soldierbot Guards and 5 Security Spider II; 160-240 items, restocks every 48 hours, open by day only.
- Recruit Sadneil can be found in the city.

## Waystation (Grey Desert)

## Slave camps (Stone Camp, South Stone Camp, Slave Farm, Port North, Port South, Eyesocket)
Also: slaver towns, slaver camps, slave camp list

## Slave Farm South, Slave Markets and Manhunter Base
Also: southern slave camps, where slaves are taken
- Slave Farm South: The Hook, about 2.0 km SSE of Clownsteady; Slave Mistress Grace. Characters enslaved by Slave Traders or Slave Hunters, and lawbreakers from Drifter's Last, are taken here.
- Escape tips: slavers bringing you from Drifter's Last must swim, cannot attack in the water and do not chase a swimmer; or wait until you are sent to work the farms outside the walls.
- Slave Markets: South Wetlands, about 2.4 km W of Catun; slaves are sold here; Slaver Bar, Used Clothing Shop, Slave Shop; no industry.
- Manhunter Base: The Hook, about 2.1 km ESE of Clownsteady; headquarters of the Manhunters; nothing but Manhunters inside.

## Mourn (town)

- Clownsteady for sale: Old Storm House (c.16,000), Longhouse (c.19,200), Y-House (c.28,000), Watchtower (c.56,000).

- Manhunter Base is about 2.1 km ESE of Clownsteady.

## Waystation (The Hook)

- Between Clownsteady (1.1 km NNE) and Drifter's Last (1.9 km SW). Faction: Tech Hunters.

## Flotsam Village (outpost)

- Flotsam Village is an outpost. Region: Hidden Forest, north of Rebirth and about 1.4 km W of World's End. Faction: Flotsam Ninjas (leader Moll), enemies of The Holy Nation.

## Spring and Free Settlement
Also: Anti-Slavers HQ

- Spring shops: Ranger Shop, Tech Hunter Shop; an Adventurer Bar per the bar page.
- Spring for sale: Metal Wallhouse x3 and Swamp House x3 (c.32,000 each).
- Wearing armour of the Anti-Slavers' enemies, or being allied with those enemies, can make them hostile.

## Holy Nation border forts (Okran's Shield, Okran's Fist, Narko's Trap)
Also: Holy Nation forts, eastern gate
- Okran's Shield: heavily fortified town on the border of Skinner's Roam and Iron Valleys, guarding the eastern approach; High Inquisitor Valtena; hospital, barracks, armory; no shops.
- Okran's Shield can be bypassed by a narrow mountain pass to the north or through Iron Valleys to the south.
- Okran's Fist: garrison on the eastern Arm of Okran guarding the north-eastern approach.
- Narko's Trap: outpost around the only ancient laboratory in Holy Nation land, about 2.8 km NE of Bad Teeth; entering is trespassing unless allied (50+ relations).
- A Holy Nation Watchtower stands near Okran's Fist; it holds food, armour and weapons, and many sleeping guards.

## Tengu's Vault and Cactus Den
Also: United Cities prison, Sand Ninjas HQ
- Tengu's Vault: military prison at the south end of Skimsands, about 2.1 km W of Heng; samurai gate guards warn, then attack trespassers; if allied to the United Cities they only warn.
- Cactus Den: watchtower in Spine Canyon about 2.0 km NW of Tengu's Vault; home of the Sand Ninjas and their leader Sand Ninja Oni; entering makes them hostile.

## Drowned Ruins (Armour King's shop)
Also: Armour King, best armour shop
- Region: Wend, midway up the river between Rebirth and the Hidden Forest.
- The Armour King, a Skeleton merchant, sells high-end armour here, guarded by Security Spider II sentries and his thralls.
- Reachable only by swimming up or down the river: enter from the south near Blister Hill or from the north-west near the Floodlands.
- Skeletons do not need to swim, so send one if Swimming is low.
- Do not steal: everyone here is extremely skilled.

## Cannibal Capital and cannibal villages
Also: cannibal towns, cannibal HQ
- Cannibal Capital: Cannibal Plains, about 3.5 km NW of Flotsam Village. It shows on the map as the neutral town "Deadcat", but it has fallen to Cannibals.
- Holds many Cannibals and Scrawny Cannibals, at least 4 Cannibal Chiefs and the Cannibal Grand Wizard.
- Captured characters are killed.
- Cannibal Villages: three in Cannibal Plains around the capital, two in Darkfinger.
- First Village: far north of Darkfinger; home of Meat Lord.

## Crab Town and Crab Villages
Also: Crab Raiders HQ, buy crabs
- Crab Town: north-east of The Pits East; capital of the Crab Raiders. They are not hostile to visitors unless you are allied with the Skeleton Bandits or Skin Bandits.
- Crab Town buildings: Crab Bar, Crab Smithy, Crab Shop, Palace of the Crab Queen; unique recruit Lumi.
- Crab Villages (Greenbeach and Stobe's Garden): Crab Smithy, Crab Shop; Crabs can be bought; beds are free to use.
- Both are deep in the dangerous south-east: Black Scratch is about 7.5 km NW of Crab Town.

- Ark: Reavers HQ, slightly north of the centre of Stobe's Garden; leader Valamon.
- Tower of Abuse: Thrall Masters, Venge; also an Ancient Tech Lab with an Ancient Science Book and AI Core.

## Fish Isle, Southern Hive and Cat-Lon's Exile
Also: end game locations, far south-east locations
- Fish Isle: ruined town on Fishman Island, about 4.0 km S of Catun; overrun by Fishmen.
- Southern Hive: Royal Valley, about 4.4 km SSE of Mourn; Queen of the South, Elite Droneguards and the unique enemy King.
- Southern Hive Villages hold many drones and Droneguards; one is about 1.5 km SW of Spring.
